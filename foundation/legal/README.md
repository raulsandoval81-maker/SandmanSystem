# Sandman Legal Documents

This folder contains the editable source-of-truth documents for Sandman legal and participation documents.

## Canonical Enrollment Waiver

Public PDFs:

- `/public/waiver/sandman-participation-waiver-en.pdf`
- `/public/waiver/sandman-participation-waiver-es.pdf`

The participation waiver is neutral.

Signer authority is handled by the intake certification:

- Parent / guardian certification for a minor athlete
- Adult athlete certification for self-signature

Older waiver variants remain in place until reviewed and intentionally archived.
