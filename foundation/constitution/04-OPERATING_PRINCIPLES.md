# Sandman Operating Principles

STATUS

LOCKED

Purpose

These principles guide every decision made within Sandman System.

Technology may change.

People may change.

Implementation may change.

These principles should remain stable.

---

# Principle 1

Mission Before Software

Sandman exists to build stronger people.

Software exists to support that mission.

Never reverse the relationship.

---

# Principle 2

Coach First

Sandman is coach-led.

Technology supports coaching.

Technology does not replace coaching.

When in doubt:

Support the coach.

---

# Principle 3

Doctrine Before Implementation

Architecture follows doctrine.

Implementation follows architecture.

Never allow implementation to become doctrine simply because it exists.

---

# Principle 4

Verify Before Building

Inspect current files.

Understand current architecture.

Confirm assumptions.

Only then begin implementation.

---

# Principle 5

Extend Before Replace

If an existing system already owns the responsibility:

Improve it.

Avoid creating parallel systems.

Replace only when necessary.

---

# Principle 6

One Responsibility

Every system should have one primary purpose.

Every page should have one primary purpose.

Every collection should have one primary purpose.

Complexity grows from blurred responsibility.

---

# Principle 7

One Source of Truth

Avoid duplicated business logic.

Avoid duplicated pricing.

Avoid duplicated routing.

Avoid duplicated authority.

Reuse shared data whenever practical.

---

# Principle 8

Authority Matters

System Admin governs.

Management operates.

Coach leads.

Families participate.

Athletes train.

Respect the hierarchy.

---

# Principle 9

Culture Before Paperwork

House Standards.

Pledge.

Community.

Purpose.

Paperwork supports participation.

It should never become the first impression.

---

# Principle 10

Simple Wins

Choose the simplest architecture that preserves long-term flexibility.

Avoid clever solutions that future coaches cannot understand.

---

# Principle 11

Build For Scale

Every major feature should ask:

Can another coach understand this?

Can another academy use this?

Can another location operate this?

If not:

Improve the design.

---

# Principle 12

Consistency Beats Perfection

Consistent experiences create trust.

Families should receive the same quality enrollment regardless of coach, location, or device.

---

# Principle 13

Protect Trust

Never surprise families.

Never hide pricing.

Never pressure enrollment.

Trust produces long-term success.

---

# Principle 14

Technology Should Disappear

The best technology feels invisible.

Families should remember the coach.

Not the software.

---

# Principle 15

Document Decisions

Important decisions belong in Foundation.

Not inside conversations.

Not inside memory.

Institutional knowledge should survive people.

---

# Principle 16

Finish Before Expanding

Complete systems before creating new ones.

Avoid collecting unfinished features.

Operational excellence comes from completion.

---

# Principle 17

Respect Existing Work

Before redesigning:

Understand why the current system exists.

Preserve good decisions.

Replace only genuine weaknesses.

---

# Principle 18

Teach Through Systems

Every workflow should teach future coaches how Sandman operates.

The software itself becomes part of coach development.

---

# Principle 19

Legacy Thinking

Build systems that still make sense ten years from now.

Avoid short-term convenience that creates long-term maintenance.

---

# Principle 20

Leave It Better

Every commit.

Every document.

Every feature.

Every conversation.

Leave Sandman clearer than it was before.
