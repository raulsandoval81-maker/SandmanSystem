# Mission Statement

Build stronger people through:

Combat • Strength • Honor

Everything within Sandman System should support this mission.

If a decision improves software but weakens the mission, reconsider the decision.

The mission is always the highest priority.

# Why Sandman Exists

Sandman exists to improve lives through structured coaching, meaningful challenge, and positive community.

Athletic performance matters.

Character matters more.

Competition matters.

Growth matters more.

Winning matters.

The person becoming stronger matters most.

# Success

Sandman is successful when:

Athletes improve.

Families trust us.
