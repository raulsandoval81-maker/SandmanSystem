# Coach Enrollment Playbook

STATUS

UNDER REVIEW

Purpose

Standardize the Sandman enrollment meeting so every family receives a consistent experience regardless of which coach conducts the meeting.

This document defines the enrollment conversation.

It is not software documentation.

It is the operational playbook.

---

# Philosophy

Families enroll with people.

Not software.

The software exists to support the coach.

The coach creates trust.

---

# Primary Objective

Help the family make the correct long-term decision.

Not simply close a sale.

The best enrollment is the right enrollment.

# Step 1

Welcome

Objectives

Build trust.

Learn names.

Reduce anxiety.

Create a positive first impression.

The first few minutes should feel conversational.

Not transactional.

---

# Step 2

Discover

Understand:

Goals

Experience

Motivation

Family expectations

Concerns

Recommended questions:

Why are you here?

What do you hope changes?

What would success look like six months from now?

Listen more than you speak.

---

# Step 3

Recommend

The coach recommends the correct path.

The family should never feel forced into choosing programs they do not understand.

Recommendations should prioritize athlete development over revenue.

---

# Step 4

Present Proposal

Review:

Programs

Schedule

Pricing

Billing

Funding (if applicable)

Expectations

Proposal should answer:

What are we doing?

Why?

How much?

When do we begin?

---

# Step 5

House Standards

Explain:

Combat

Strength

Honor

Review academy expectations.

These are not legal documents.

They establish culture.

---

# Step 6

Questions

Invite questions.

Address concerns honestly.

Never pressure.

Families should understand both benefits and expectations before enrolling.

---

# Step 7

Decision

If the answer is yes:

Proceed confidently.

If the answer is no:

Leave the relationship positive.

Follow-up remains possible.

Never burn trust.

---

# Step 8

Stripe

Collect payment.

Stripe executes payment.

Proposal remains the business agreement.

Payment confirms commitment.

---

# Step 9

Athlete & Family Pledge

Review the Sandman Pledge.

This represents commitment to the culture of the academy.

The pledge follows payment.

---

# Step 10

Coach-approved Intake

Coach initiates the intake process.

Required information includes:

Medical

Emergency

Waiver

Participation information

Other operational requirements

---

# Step 11

Enrollment Complete

Welcome the athlete.

Explain next steps.

First practice.

Uniform information.

Communication.

Parent Portal.

Coach expectations.

End with excitement.

Not paperwork.

---

# Guiding Principles

Coach first.

Family focused.

No pressure.

No surprises.

No hidden pricing.

No confusion.

Build trust.

Build relationships.

Represent Sandman professionally.

---

# Success Metric

A successful enrollment is not measured only by payment.

It is measured by:

Family confidence.

Athlete excitement.

Coach confidence.

Operational readiness.

Long-term retention.
