# Purpose

Enrollment transitions a prospect from an accepted Proposal into an active Sandman athlete.

Enrollment is not a payment page.

Enrollment is not Intake.

Enrollment coordinates the closing process.

---

# Objectives

• Standardize coach-led enrollments.

• Create a repeatable closing system.

• Preserve Sandman culture.

• Prepare the athlete for operational onboarding.

---

# Enrollment Philosophy

The coach remains responsible.

The software guides.

The family participates.

---

# Responsibilities

Enrollment owns:

• House Standards

• Closing guidance

• Payment handoff

• Athlete / Family Pledge

• Intake initiation

Enrollment does NOT own:

• Proposal generation

• Pricing logic

• XP

• Athlete progression

• Attendance

---

# House Standards

Purpose

Introduce Sandman culture.

This is not a legal agreement.

This explains:

Combat

Strength

Honor

Coach expectations

Academy expectations

---

# Stripe

Purpose

Collect payment.

Stripe executes payment.

Proposal remains the agreement.

Payment should be confirmed before operational onboarding continues.

---

# Athlete / Family Pledge

Purpose

Personal commitment.

Occurs after payment.

Separate from:

House Standards

Waiver

Intake

---

# Intake

Purpose

Collect operational information.

Includes:

Medical

Emergency

Waiver

Participation information

Required acknowledgements

Coach controls intake authorization.

---

# Coach Review
