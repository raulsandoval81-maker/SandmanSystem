# Intake Architecture

STATUS

UNDER REVIEW

Purpose

Intake transitions an enrolled participant into an operational athlete record.

Intake is not Enrollment.

Intake is not Admissions.

Intake is operational onboarding.

---

# Philosophy

Enrollment creates commitment.

Intake creates readiness.

The athlete should not become operational until Intake is complete.

---

# Objectives

Collect information required for:

• Safe coaching

• Communication

• Emergency response

• Legal participation

• Operational setup

---

# Canonical Flow

Enrollment Complete

↓

Coach Approval

↓

Parent / Adult Athlete Intake

↓

Coach Review

↓

Mint

↓

Operational Athlete

---

# Intake Types

Parent Intake

Purpose

Guardian completes required information for a minor athlete.

---

Adult Athlete Intake

Purpose

Adult athlete completes their own information.

---

Coach Intake

Purpose

Coach review, verification, activation.

Coach Intake is not a duplicate intake.

It is the internal review workflow.

---

# Shared Components

Every intake should reuse shared logic whenever possible.

Examples

Validation

Medical
