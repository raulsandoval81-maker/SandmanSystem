# Sandman Coach communication boundaries

Status: Locked decision — October 11, 2026.

## Channels
- Coach ↔ athlete: instruction, correction, and discussion in the training room. No individual digital direct-message channel in either direction.
- Coach ↔ parent/guardian: direct conversations in Para-Comms, using established authorized family threads.
- Coach → team: authorized Para-Comms broadcast and announcements, including athlete groups.
- Athlete development: assessment, skills, progression, testing, and recognition are structured records, not private messages.

## Coach Home ownership
- Athlete Operations: development work, never a personal message inbox or message badge.
- Team Operations: Para-Comms messages requiring Coach attention; count only authorized unread conversations. No count when zero, unavailable, or not connected.
- A broadcast or pinned announcement does not create an unread Coach message.
- Do not double-count a conversation under Athlete and Team.

## Future Coach rule
All Coaches inherit the same restrictions. Do not introduce a private athlete DM, athlete-to-Coach reply route, or athlete-specific one-way note under the label of communication without a new explicit safety and governance review. Parent/guardian access, auditability, role permissions, and age-specific safeguards must be reviewed before changing this boundary.

This decision does not prohibit ordinary in-room coaching or the existing structured development workflow.
