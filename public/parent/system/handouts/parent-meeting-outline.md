# Sandman Parent Meeting Outline

## 1. Welcome and purpose

- Introduce the family partnership around athlete development.
- Confirm that safety, growth, consistency, and clear communication are shared priorities.

## 2. The Sandman framework

- Combat: technical skill and decision-making.
- Strength: physical capacity and durable training habits.
- Honor: character, reflection, accountability, and leadership.
- Explain that available lanes and assignments depend on the athlete's current program and status.

## 3. Training expectations

- Preparation, punctuality, attention, effort, and respect.
- Safe conduct and prompt communication about injuries or restrictions.
- Appropriate family support without comparison or pressure.

## 4. XP and progression

- XP records approved participation and development; it is not a race or a promise of promotion.
- Readiness includes demonstrated standards and coach evaluation.
- Testing, passing, any required waiting period, and promotion are distinct steps.
- Refer families to the athlete's current profile for current status and program-specific requirements.

## 5. Communication and recognition

- Review the appropriate channels for absences, questions, and private concerns.
- Explain that ceremonies recognize progress already earned; event details are communicated separately.

## 6. Questions and next steps

- Confirm where the family can find Parent System resources.
- Confirm the athlete's current program, assigned discipline, and current access.
- Record questions that require follow-up from the appropriate Sandman staff member.
