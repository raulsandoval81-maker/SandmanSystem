Who It Is For

People Ready To Start The Day Strong

Dawn Patrol is designed for adults who want a structured, high-energy workout before work, school, family responsibilities, or the demands of the day.

Movements can be adjusted to different fitness levels while preserving the structure and purpose of the workout.
