Promotions

Review promotional offers and seasonal campaign interest in one Management workspace.

How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Review introductory offers and season-specific interest without confusing promotional requests with the formal admissions lifecycle.

What you do here

Review each request, confirm the promotion or visit type, update its status, respond appropriately, and route the family into the formal admissions process when the interaction becomes a real enrollment opportunity.

What happens next

Qualified families continue through the appropriate Management path, normally Lead or Appointment, then Admissions Decision, Prospect Builder, Proposal, Review & Confirm, payment, and Enrollment as applicable.

Authority

The family initiates the promotional request. Management owns review, response, status, and routing into formal admissions. Promotional access does not independently authorize enrollment, payment, roster placement, or athlete progression.

Important

Promotional requests support admissions but are not canonical lifecycle stages. Do not use a promotional status as a substitute for proposal acceptance, payment, Intake, or athlete activation.
