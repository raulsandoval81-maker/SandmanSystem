How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Manage issued family proposals from client review and proposal acceptance through secure Review & Confirm, checkout, payment, and the paid enrollment handoff.

What you do here

Open the case that matches the current proposal stage, use the appropriate Review & Confirm path, and move the family to its next approved step. Correct the proposal when the offer itself needs to change.

What happens next

After proposal acceptance, the family reviews and confirms the locked enrollment and billing details, then continues to Stripe. Once payment is confirmed, the case continues to Management Enrollment for the controlled Intake handoff.

Authority

Management owns proposal issuance, safeguards, and workflow progression. The family owns proposal acceptance and Review & Confirm. Stripe owns payment confirmation. Management resumes authority after confirmed payment for the Enrollment handoff.

Important

Proposal acceptance, Review & Confirm, payment, and Intake are separate stages. Manual proposal acceptance does not replace Review & Confirm or payment, and the Proposal workspace does not complete waiver, medical, emergency, athlete activation, or starting XP.

Management Operating Rules Workflow Guide
Review & Confirm Actions
  • In-Person: Opens the Review & Confirm page for use with the family.
  • Remote: Sends the secure Review & Confirm link by email.
  • Alternative Secure Link: Copies the secure link for a manual handoff.
Workflow

Lead → Appointment → Proposal → Review & Confirm → Stripe → Management Enrollment → Intake → Activation → Coach Assessment → Management Validation.

Payment
  • Due Now is the amount scheduled for the initial checkout.
  • Monthly membership is the recurring membership responsibility.
  • Additional unpaid monthly periods are entered only when Management has confirmed they are owed.
  • Stripe is the payment authority for successful checkout/payment confirmation.
Management: Edit Proposal
  • Use when the offer itself needs to be changed.
  • Editing returns the proposal to the controlled proposal-building workflow.
  • A changed offer must return through the appropriate family review and confirmation path.
Management Enrollment
  • Payment does not automatically complete Intake or athlete activation.
  • Management owns the secure Intake handoff, Intake review, and activation.
  • The family or adult athlete supplies Intake information and required acknowledgements.
Coach Assessment & Management Validation
  • Coach assessment happens after the Management enrollment handoff.
  • Coach assessment informs technical placement and development.
  • Management validates authoritative starting placement and starting XP where applicable.
Safeguards & Audit
  • Manual acceptance is a safeguard for valid signed acceptance already on file; it does not replace Review & Confirm or payment.
  • Alternative handoffs should preserve the same secure Review & Confirm destination.
  • Keep proposal corrections and payment history auditable.
