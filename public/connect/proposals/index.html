How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Manage issued family proposals from client review and proposal acceptance through guardian Review & Confirm, the correct payment route, and the enrollment handoff.

What you do here

Open the case that matches the current proposal stage, use the appropriate Review & Confirm path, and move the family to its next approved step. Correct the proposal when the offer itself needs to change.

What happens next

After proposal acceptance, the guardian reviews and confirms the locked enrollment terms in person or by secure email link. Unpaid online arrangements continue to Stripe. Existing Cash/Check Paid-in-Full arrangements go to Management payment reconciliation without a second charge. Once payment is verified, Management controls the Intake handoff.

Authority

Management owns proposal issuance, safeguards, payment reconciliation, and workflow progression. The guardian personally completes Review & Confirm by either delivery method. Stripe confirms online payments only; Management verifies historical cash/check deposits. Intake and activation require their own approvals.

Important

Proposal acceptance, guardian Review & Confirm, payment verification, and Intake are separate stages. Neither in-person nor email delivery authorizes staff to confirm on the guardian's behalf. Existing payment is never charged again; the Proposal workspace does not complete waiver, medical, emergency, athlete activation, or starting XP.

Management Operating Rules Workflow Guide
Review & Confirm Actions
  • In Person: Opens the secure Review & Confirm page for the guardian to approve personally with staff present.
  • Email: Sends the same secure Review & Confirm page to the guardian for remote approval.
  • Alternative Secure Link: Copies the secure link for a manual handoff.
Workflow

Lead → Appointment → Proposal → Guardian Review & Confirm (in person or email) → Online payment via Stripe OR existing Cash/Check reconciliation by Management → Intake → Activation → Coach Assessment → Management Validation.

Payment
  • Due Now is the amount scheduled for the initial checkout.
  • Monthly membership is the recurring membership responsibility.
  • Additional unpaid monthly periods are entered only when Management has confirmed they are owed.
  • Stripe confirms unpaid online checkout only. Cash/Check Paid-in-Full requires genuine prior-payment evidence and Management reconciliation, never a second checkout.
Management: Edit Proposal
  • Use when the offer itself needs to be changed.
  • Editing returns the proposal to the controlled proposal-building workflow.
  • A changed offer must return through the appropriate family review and confirmation path.
Management Enrollment
  • Payment does not automatically complete Intake or athlete activation.
  • Management owns the secure Intake handoff, Intake review, and activation.
  • The family or adult athlete supplies Intake information and required acknowledgements.
Coach Assessment & Management Validation
  • Coach assessment happens after the Management enrollment handoff.
  • Coach assessment informs technical placement and development.
  • Management validates authoritative starting placement and starting XP where applicable.
Safeguards & Audit
  • Manual acceptance is a safeguard for valid signed acceptance already on file; it does not replace Review & Confirm or payment.
  • Alternative handoffs should preserve the same secure Review & Confirm destination.
  • Keep proposal corrections and payment history auditable.
