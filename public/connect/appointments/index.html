How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Schedule and manage the Admissions Appointment that gives Management the information needed for the formal admissions decision.

What you do here

Review the selected lead, choose the appointment date and time, confirm the visit, send the appointment confirmation, and coordinate Coach technical verification when prior experience requires it.

What happens next

After the appointment, Management records the result in Admissions Decision. Families approved to continue can then move into the Prospect Builder and Proposal process.

Authority

Management owns appointment scheduling, confirmation, and admissions coordination. The family confirms attendance and provides information. The Coach owns only requested technical evaluation, not the admissions disposition.

Important

Management owns appointment scheduling and admissions handling. The Coach owns technical verification when requested. This page does not itself approve the final membership offer, collect payment, complete Intake, or establish starting XP.
