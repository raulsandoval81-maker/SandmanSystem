Purpose

Turn an interested family into a confirmed Admissions Appointment.

What you do here

What happens next

After the appointment, Management records the outcome and moves qualified families toward the Prospect Builder and Proposal process.

Important

Management owns scheduling and admissions handling. The Coach owns technical verification of claimed experience when requested.
