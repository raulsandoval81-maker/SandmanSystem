How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Review new family interest and decide the correct admissions route before the case advances.

What you do here

Review the family and program interest, confirm contact information, follow up as needed, and normally schedule the appropriate Admissions Appointment. Use the approved Walk-In Enrollment path only when the case legitimately qualifies to bypass appointment scheduling.

What happens next

A normally scheduled family moves into Appointments. An approved walk-in case can move directly into the pricing and proposal path while retaining its admissions history.

Authority

The family supplies interest and contact information. Management owns lead triage and routing. Coach authority begins only when technical experience or readiness must be evaluated.

Important

Lead review records interest and routes the case. It does not perform Coach technical assessment, finalize pricing, collect payment, complete Intake, or award starting XP.
