How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Manage Parent and Athlete login access for a member who already exists in Sandman.

What you do here

Search by Athlete ID or name, review Parent and Athlete access status, send or resend first-time access, record Parent approval for direct Athlete access under age 14 when required, and send password recovery for an active account.

What happens next

The member uses the issued or recovered account to access the appropriate Sandman experience. Any change to enrollment, membership, billing, athlete progression, or XP continues in its owning Management workflow.

Important

Members manages account access only. It does not recreate enrollment, change membership or pricing, alter rank or XP, change athlete progression, or replace the athlete record.
