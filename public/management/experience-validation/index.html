Experience Validation

Review Coach-verified prior experience and finalize starting recognition.

How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Finalize Management recognition of prior experience after the Coach has completed the required technical validation.

What you do here

Review the submitted experience claim and Coach-verified recommendation, confirm the evidence and scope, and record the Management decision for starting recognition.

What happens next

Approved recognition can inform the athlete’s starting placement and catch-up path. Any authoritative starting XP or placement is still recorded through the Management-controlled progression process.

Authority

The athlete or family may claim prior experience. The Coach evaluates and returns the technical recommendation. Management verifies the returned assessment, authorizes the canonical recognition, and controls any resulting starting-placement or catch-up XP record.

Important

The Coach evaluates technical experience; Management authorizes the canonical result. This page is not a shortcut around testing, promotion, or normal earned XP.
