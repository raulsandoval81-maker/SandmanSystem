Review checkout, payment, pass, and enrollment follow-through.

How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Monitor checkout and payment status so Management can resume the correct financial or enrollment step.

What you do here

Review cases that need action, are waiting, are paid and ready, or are closed; open the relevant case; and resolve the next billing follow-through.

What happens next

Incomplete checkout remains in the proposal or billing workflow. Confirmed paid cases continue to Management Enrollment for the controlled Intake handoff.

Authority

Stripe or the approved payment-recording workflow establishes payment state. Management monitors and resolves follow-through but does not manually substitute a different lifecycle stage for confirmed payment. Paid cases return to Management for Enrollment.

Important

Billing tracks financial state and follow-through. It does not replace proposal acceptance, customer Review & Confirm, Intake, athlete activation, membership access management, or XP administration.
