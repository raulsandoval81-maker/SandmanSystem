How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Find a family or athlete case, understand where it is in the lifecycle, and resume work in the correct operational workspace.

What you do here

Search for the case, review its current stage and activity history, and open the appropriate destination when follow-up is required.

What happens next

Work continues in the workspace that owns the current stage, such as Leads, Appointments, Proposals, Billing, Enrollment, or Members.

Authority

Pipeline History reflects recorded lifecycle state. Management may use it to understand and resume a case, but authority to perform the next action remains with the canonical workspace that owns that stage.

Important

Pipeline History is an audit and navigation surface. It should not become a second place to perform lifecycle actions that belong to another canonical workspace.
