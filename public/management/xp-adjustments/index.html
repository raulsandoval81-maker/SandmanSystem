XP Management

Open a returned Coach assessment directly, or find an existing athlete when a legitimate administrative XP correction is required.

How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Manage returned Coach experience validation and legitimate administrative XP corrections from one athlete-first Management workspace.

Experience Validation

Coach evaluates claimed prior experience and returns the technical recommendation. Management approves or rejects recognition, then records final placement.

1 Year: 200 XP now · 2 Years: 200 now + 200 held · 3+ Years: 300 now + 300 held.

XP Adjustments

Use only for a verified administrative need such as Delayed Onboarding, Downtime Recovery, Paper Reconciliation, or Correction.

Authority

Management finalizes experience recognition and administrative XP corrections. Adjustments remain separate from normal earned XP, testing, promotion, Strength, Honor, and competition awards.

What happens next

Find the athlete in Step 1, confirm the correct athlete and discipline in Step 2, then complete any applicable experience validation or XP correction in Step 3.
