How this page works Instructions
Purpose

Review location-scoped attendance and participation history from finalized practice records.

What you do here

Monitor finalized-session totals, participation, pending review counts, daily attendance patterns, discipline participation, and recent finalized sessions.

What happens next

Use the report to understand operations and identify follow-up, then return to the owning workspace when a correction or action is required.

Authority

The system summarizes finalized source records. Management uses Reports for visibility and decisions, but corrections must be made in the workspace that owns the underlying record.

Important

Reports are read-oriented operational summaries. Corrections belong in the source workflow, such as Coach attendance review or the appropriate Management tool.
