Muay Thai Skill Tree 1 — Prospect

12 skills • Road2Champion Muay Thai curriculum stage T1

Prospect

Skill 1 — One Two

A — Flow jab to cross with balance and rhythm

B — Maintain guard and stance during the combination

C — Finish the exchange under control and reset

Skill 2 — Lead Hook

A — Deliver a compact lead hook with balance and rotation

B — Maintain defensive structure during the strike

C — Recover immediately into stance after contact

Skill 3 — Rear Hook

A — Rotate hips and shoulders to generate rear hook power

B — Keep stance and guard disciplined during rotation

C — Recover balance immediately after the strike

Skill 4 — Uppercut

A — Drive the uppercut upward with hips and posture

B — Maintain balance and defensive awareness during close exchanges

C — Recover quickly into stance after contact

Skill 5 — Body Shot

A — Attack the body with balance and proper level change

B — Maintain defensive awareness while entering range

C — Recover immediately into stance after striking

Skill 6 — Lead Kick

A — Deliver a controlled lead kick with speed and balance

B — Maintain defensive posture during the strike

C — Recover quickly into stance after contact

Skill 7 — Rear Kick

A — Generate controlled power through rear kick rotation

B — Maintain posture and balance through the strike

C — Recover stance immediately after impact

Skill 8 — Teep

A — Use the teep to manage pressure and distance

B — Maintain balance and defensive posture during extension

C — Reset immediately after contact

Skill 9 — Check and Return System

A — Read and check the kick with posture

B — Replace the checking foot in stance

C — Return safely before the opponent resets

Skill 10 — Block

A — Defend punches and strikes with controlled blocks

B — Maintain stance and vision during defensive exchanges

C — Recover immediately into movement or counters

Skill 11 — Angle

A — Create better attacking and defensive positions through angles

B — Move off the center line while maintaining stance and balance

C — Recover into offense or defense immediately after movement

Skill 12 — Basic Combo

A — Blend punches, kicks, and movement into controlled combinations

B — Maintain stance, guard, and balance during exchanges

C — Recover and reset cleanly after combinations
