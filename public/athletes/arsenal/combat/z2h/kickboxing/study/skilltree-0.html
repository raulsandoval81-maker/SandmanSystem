Road2Champion™ • Youth Journey

Muay Thai Skill Tree 0 — Shadow

12 skills • Road2Champion Muay Thai curriculum stage T0

Shadow

Skill 1 — Stance

A — Build an athletic Muay Thai stance with balance and posture

B — Keep hands ready, chin protected, and feet under control

C — Reset stance after movement, strikes, or contact

Skill 2 — Guard

A — Protect chin, head, and body with disciplined hand position

B — Return hands after every strike

C — Keep guard active while moving, kicking, or defending

Skill 3 — Footwork

A — Move forward, backward, and side-to-side without crossing feet

B — Keep stance while entering and exiting range

C — Reset balance before striking or defending

Skill 4 — Jab

A — Fire the lead hand straight from stance

B — Use the jab to manage distance and start offense

C — Return immediately to guard and stance

Skill 5 — Cross

A — Drive the rear hand straight through the target

B — Rotate hip, shoulder, and rear foot together

C — Recover back to stance and guard after impact

Skill 6 — Hook

A — Deliver a tight hook with balance and rotation

B — Keep elbow aligned and strike from proper range

C — Return to guard immediately after impact

Skill 7 — Teep Introduction

A — Lift the knee with upright posture

B — Extend the foot to manage distance with control

C — Return directly to balanced stance

Skill 8 — Round Kick

A — Rotate hips and shoulders through the kick

B — Strike with proper shin and body alignment

C — Recover balance and return to stance after impact

Skill 9 — Check

A — Lift the leg to defend incoming kicks safely

B — Maintain balance and guard during the defense

C — Recover immediately back to stance and readiness

Skill 10 — Distance

A — Understand safe striking and defensive range

B — Enter and exit distance with control

C — Stay balanced while managing space

Skill 11 — Pad Work

A — Strike pads with control, balance, and proper mechanics

B — Listen and respond to coaching commands during rounds

C — Maintain pace, posture, and composure through work periods

Skill 12 — Foundation Evaluation

A — Demonstrate balanced stance, guard, and basic movement

B — Show the basic punches, kicks, teep, check, and distance skills introduced in T0

C — Complete simple coach-directed pad work with control and return to stance
