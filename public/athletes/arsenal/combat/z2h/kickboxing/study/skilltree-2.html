Muay Thai Skill Tree 2

12 skills • Road2Champion Muay Thai curriculum stage T2

Prospect Foundation

Skill 1 — Timing

A — Recognize openings and strike at the correct moment

B — Blend movement, rhythm, and reactions together

C — Maintain balance and composure during exchanges

Skill 2 — Rhythm

A — Control pace and movement through rhythm changes

B — Blend offense and defense fluidly during exchanges

C — Maintain composure under live movement

Skill 3 — Range Control

A — Control striking distance through movement and pressure

B — Blend offense and defense while managing range

C — Maintain composure during live exchanges

Skill 4 — Pivot

A — Create angles and escape pressure through pivots

B — Maintain balance and stance during directional movement

C — Recover immediately into offense or defense after movement

Skill 5 — Exit Angle

A — Exit exchanges by moving off the center line

B — Maintain stance, guard, and balance while leaving range

C — Reset into position for defense or counter offense

Skill 6 — Defense Reset

A — Complete the defense with balanced posture

B — Rebuild guard and stance before the next exchange

C — Return safely with a strike, angle, or controlled exit

Competitor Application

Skill 7 — Feint

A — Create reactions using believable movement and setup

B — Blend feints naturally into offense and entries

C — Stay balanced and ready to capitalize on reactions

Skill 8 — Counter

A — Recognize openings after defensive reactions

B — Return offense immediately with balance and control

C — Recover stance and defense after counter exchanges

Skill 9 — Jab Kick Entry

A — Blend punches into kick entries fluidly

B — Maintain balance and defensive posture during transitions

C — Recover stance after combinations and exits

Skill 10 — Cross Kick Entry

A — Use rear hand offense to set up kick entries

B — Maintain stance and defensive awareness through transitions

C — Recover balance after striking sequences

Skill 11 — Hook Kick Counter

A — Counter with hook-to-kick transitions under pressure

B — Blend defensive reactions into offense fluidly

C — Maintain composure and balance during counters

Skill 12 — Combination Flow

A — Connect punches and kicks without losing posture

B — Change range while staying balanced

C — Finish in guard with a safe angle or exit
