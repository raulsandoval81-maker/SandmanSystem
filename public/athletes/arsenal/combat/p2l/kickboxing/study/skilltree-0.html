Muay Thai Skill Tree 0 — Apprentice

20 skills • Path2Legend Muay Thai curriculum stage T0

Apprentice

Skill 1 — Stance

A — Build an athletic kickboxing stance with balance and posture

B — Keep hands ready, chin protected, and feet under control

C — Reset stance after movement, strikes, or contact

Skill 2 — Guard

A — Protect chin, head, and body with disciplined hand position

B — Return hands after every strike

C — Keep guard active while moving, kicking, or defending

Skill 3 — Footwork

A — Move forward, backward, and side-to-side without crossing feet

B — Keep stance while entering and exiting range

C — Reset balance before striking or defending

Skill 4 — Jab

A — Fire the lead hand straight from stance

B — Use the jab to manage distance and start offense

C — Return immediately to guard and stance

Skill 5 — Cross

A — Drive the rear hand straight through the target

B — Rotate hip, shoulder, and rear foot together

C — Recover back to stance and guard after impact

Skill 6 — Hook

A — Deliver a tight hook with balance and rotation

B — Keep elbow aligned and strike from proper range

C — Return to guard immediately after impact

Skill 7 — Front Kick

A — Lift knee first and extend the kick straight forward

B — Strike with balance and controlled posture

C — Recover the leg quickly back to stance

Skill 8 — Round Kick

A — Rotate hips and shoulders through the kick

B — Strike with proper shin and body alignment

C — Recover balance and return to stance after impact

Skill 9 — Check

A — Lift the leg to defend incoming kicks safely

B — Maintain balance and guard during the defense

C — Recover immediately back to stance and readiness

Skill 10 — Knee

A — Drive the knee upward with posture and balance

B — Use hips and core to generate controlled power

C — Return quickly to stance after the strike

Skill 11 — Teep

A — Use the teep to manage distance and disrupt pressure

B — Drive from the hips while maintaining balance

C — Recover quickly back to stance after extension

Skill 12 — Basic Clinch

A — Establish safe head and hand position in the clinch

B — Maintain posture and balance while controlling contact

C — Exit the clinch under control and reset stance

Skill 13 — Distance

A — Understand safe striking and defensive range

B — Enter and exit distance with control

C — Stay balanced while managing space

Skill 14 — Angle Step

A — Move off the center line with controlled footwork

B — Maintain stance and vision while creating angles

C — Reset into offensive or defensive position after movement

Skill 15 — Basic Combination

A — Combine basic punches and kicks with flow

B — Maintain stance, balance, and guard during combinations

C — Finish combinations under control and reset

Skill 16 — Pad Work

A — Strike pads with control, balance, and proper mechanics

B — Listen and respond to coaching commands during rounds

C — Maintain pace, posture, and composure through work periods

Skill 17 — Heavy Bag

A — Strike the bag with balance, control, and clean mechanics

B — Maintain stance, guard, and breathing during work rounds

C — Finish each exchange with recovery and reset

Skill 18 — Partner Drill

A — Work safely with a partner using control and awareness

B — Follow drill rules, spacing, and coach commands

C — Maintain discipline, respect, and composure through contact

Skill 19 — Conditioning

A — Build work capacity through kickboxing-specific effort

B — Maintain stance, guard, and breathing while tired

C — Finish rounds with discipline instead of sloppy movement

Skill 20 — Controlled Sparring

A — Apply basic kickboxing skills with control and safety

B — Maintain distance, guard, stance, and composure under pressure

C — Respect partner, rules, and coach direction during live work
