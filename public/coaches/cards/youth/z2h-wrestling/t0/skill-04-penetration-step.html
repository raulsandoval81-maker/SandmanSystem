Skill 4 — Penetration Step

Tree: Stance & Motion

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Level Change First

B — Step Between the Feet

C — Bring the Trail Leg Up

Coaching Cues

  • “Level, then step”
  • “Stay tall through the chest”
  • “Bring your feet back underneath you”

Common Mistakes

  • Stepping before the level change
  • Leaning too far forward
  • Leaving the trail leg behind

Drill Ideas

  • Controlled penetration-step reps
  • Level change to penetration step
  • Step and recover to stance

Progression

  • Connect the penetration step to double-leg mechanics
