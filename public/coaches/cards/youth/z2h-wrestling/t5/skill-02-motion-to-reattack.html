Skill 2 — Motion to Reattack

Tree: Stance & Motion

Tier: Youth T5 — Champion

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Move to Create a Reaction

B — Recognize the Opening

C — Reattack With a Second Attack

Coaching Cues

  • “Move him before you attack again”
  • “Read the reaction, then go”
  • “Recover your stance and attack the opening”

Common Mistakes

  • Moving without watching the opponent’s reaction
  • Pausing after the first opening or defended attack
  • Reattacking while reaching or out of stance

Drill Ideas

  • Motion-to-reaction entry reps
  • First attack → partner blocks → second attack
  • Controlled reattack-window rounds

Progression

  • Connect to tempo change
