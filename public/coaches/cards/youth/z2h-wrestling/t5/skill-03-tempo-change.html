Skill 3 — Tempo Change

Tree: Stance & Motion

Tier: Youth T5 — Champion

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Establish a Controlled Rhythm

B — Change Pace or Pause

C — Attack the Created Opening

Coaching Cues

  • “Change the pace, not your stance”
  • “Pause to draw the reaction”
  • “Accelerate when the opening appears”

Common Mistakes

  • Changing speed without a tactical reason
  • Standing up or crossing feet during the pace change
  • Using the same slow-fast pattern every time

Drill Ideas

  • Coach-called pace-change movement
  • Motion → pause → attack reps
  • Controlled partner-reaction rounds

Progression

  • Connect to chain wrestling
