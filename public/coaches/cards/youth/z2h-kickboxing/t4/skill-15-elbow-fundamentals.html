Skill 15 — Elbow Fundamentals

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T4 — Contender

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Build a safe elbow line from balanced posture

B — Protect with the opposite hand and use coach-held pads only

C — Return to guard without uncontrolled partner contact

Coaching Cues

  • “Short path, strong posture.”
  • “Other hand stays home.”
  • “Pad target only.”
  • “Reset under control.”

Common Mistakes

  • Swinging the elbow too wide
  • Dropping the opposite hand
  • Losing posture during rotation
  • Using uncontrolled partner contact

Drill Ideas

  • Elbow-line shadow repetitions
  • Coach-held elbow pad mechanics
  • Controlled frame-to-elbow positioning without head contact
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
