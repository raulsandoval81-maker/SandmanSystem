Skill 13 — Rope Escapes

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T4 — Contender

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Escape pressure safely from ropes or corners

B — Blend movement, defense, and angles under pressure

C — Recover position and composure after escapes

Coaching Cues

  • “Don’t stay trapped.”
  • “Angle out.”
  • “Defend while moving.”
  • “Recover immediately.”

Common Mistakes

  • Backing straight into pressure
  • Freezing near boundaries
  • Escaping without defense
  • Standing square after exits

Drill Ideas

  • Corner escape rounds
  • Pressure-exit controlled partner work
  • Defensive movement drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
