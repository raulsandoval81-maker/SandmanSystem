Skill 8 — Round Kick

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Rotate hips and shoulders through the kick

B — Strike with proper shin and body alignment

C — Recover balance and return to stance after impact

Coaching Cues

  • “Turn the hip over.”
  • “Whip the kick.”
  • “Hands stay active.”
  • “Land balanced.”

Common Mistakes

  • Kicking with the foot instead of shin
  • Not pivoting the base foot
  • Dropping hands
  • Falling after the kick

Drill Ideas

  • Round kick shadow rounds
  • Pad kick repetition
  • Kick-return movement drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
