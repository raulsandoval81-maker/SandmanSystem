Skill 12 — Foundation Evaluation

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Demonstrate balanced stance, guard, and basic movement

B — Show the basic punches, kicks, teep, check, and distance skills introduced in T0

C — Complete simple coach-directed pad work with control and return to stance

Coaching Cues

  • “Start from stance.”
  • “Guard stays ready.”
  • “Balance before speed.”
  • “Finish and reset.”

Common Mistakes

  • Losing stance or balance during basic movement
  • Dropping the guard after a strike
  • Rushing the basic skills instead of showing clean mechanics
  • Forgetting to reset after pad work or movement

Drill Ideas

  • Coach-called stance, guard, and movement review
  • Basic strike and return-to-stance pad review
  • Teep, round kick, check, and distance review
  • Simple controlled combinations on coach-held pads
