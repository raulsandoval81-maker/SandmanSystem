Skill 7 — Teep Introduction

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Lift the knee with upright posture

B — Extend the foot to manage distance with control

C — Return directly to balanced stance

Coaching Cues

  • “Knee first.”
  • “Kick through the target.”
  • “Stay balanced.”
  • “Bring it back fast.”

Common Mistakes

  • Dropping hands during the kick
  • Leaning backward too far
  • Pushing without chambering
  • Falling out of stance

Drill Ideas

  • Front kick air reps
  • Pad front kick rounds
  • Kick-reset movement drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
