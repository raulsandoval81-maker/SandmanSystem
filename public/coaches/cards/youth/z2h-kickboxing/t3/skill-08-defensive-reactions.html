Skill 8 — Defensive Reactions

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Defend naturally under pressure and live exchanges

B — Blend movement, guard, checks, and counters together

C — Recover immediately into balanced position

Coaching Cues

  • “React without panic.”
  • “Stay composed.”
  • “Defend and move.”
  • “Recover fast.”

Common Mistakes

  • Freezing during pressure
  • Closing eyes during exchanges
  • Dropping guard while reacting
  • Standing square after defense

Drill Ideas

  • Reaction defense rounds
  • Pressure-defense partner drills
  • Defend-and-return controlled partner work
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
