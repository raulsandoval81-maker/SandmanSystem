Skill 5 — Controlled Partner Application

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Follow the coach-assigned action and contact rule

B — Apply one skill while protecting the partner

C — Reset calmly after each exchange

Coaching Cues

  • “Think while fighting.”
  • “Control before power.”
  • “Stay composed.”
  • “Use your skills.”

Common Mistakes

  • Panicking during exchanges
  • Throwing wild combinations
  • Forgetting defense under pressure
  • Losing posture when tired

Drill Ideas

  • Controlled technical rounds
  • Reaction controlled partner work
  • Pressure adaptation drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
