Skill 6 — Defense Reset

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T2 — Road2Champion Bridge

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Complete the defense with balanced posture

B — Rebuild guard and stance before the next exchange

C — Return safely with a strike, angle, or controlled exit

Coaching Cues

  • “Defense finishes in stance.”
  • “Guard first, then answer.”
  • “Eyes forward.”
  • “Reset before you re-enter.”

Common Mistakes

  • Admiring the defense instead of resetting
  • Returning while off balance
  • Dropping the guard during the exit
  • Turning away from the partner

Drill Ideas

  • Defense-to-stance repetitions
  • Check, cover, or parry followed by a controlled return
  • Partner cue reset rounds
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
