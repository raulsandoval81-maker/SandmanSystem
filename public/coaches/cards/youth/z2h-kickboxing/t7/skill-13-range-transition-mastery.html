Skill 13 — Range Transition Mastery

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Move cleanly between kicking, punching, and clinch range

B — Keep the feet underneath every weapon

C — Exit or hold position without crossing

Coaching Cues

  • “Move your base.”
  • “Don’t cross your feet.”
  • “Balance before strikes.”
  • “Exit clean.”

Common Mistakes

  • Crossing feet during pressure
  • Standing flat-footed
  • Overreaching into range
  • Moving without guard

Drill Ideas

  • Movement ladder rounds
  • Angle-and-reset drills
  • Range management partner rounds
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
