Skill 11 — Stance Ownership

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Adjust stance for punching, kicking, checking, and clinch range

B — Maintain posture through every transition

C — Recover the preferred base independently

Coaching Cues

  • “Balanced and ready.”
  • “Hands protect first.”
  • “Stay athletic.”
  • “Recover your base.”

Common Mistakes

  • Standing too square
  • Losing stance during combinations
  • Crossing feet under pressure
  • Standing too tall

Drill Ideas

  • Movement and reset rounds
  • Stance recovery drills
  • Partner pressure movement drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
