Skill 20 — Controlled Integration

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Apply the assigned system under controlled resistance

B — Adapt while protecting the training partner

C — Explain the decision and reset with composure

Coaching Cues

  • “Everything connects.”
  • “Trust your reactions.”
  • “Stay composed under pressure.”
  • “Fight with structure.”

Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting fundamentals during pressure
  • Overcommitting emotionally
  • Breaking stance during exchanges
  • Losing tactical awareness

Drill Ideas

  • Technical controlled partner work rounds
  • Pressure adaptation drills
  • Live tactical scenarios
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
