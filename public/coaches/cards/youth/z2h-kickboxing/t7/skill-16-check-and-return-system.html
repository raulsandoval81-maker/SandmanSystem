Skill 16 — Check and Return Mastery

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Recognize the incoming attack and choose the appropriate defensive response

B — Recover balance and position immediately after the defense

C — Select a controlled return, angle, or reset based on position

Coaching Cues

  • “Read before you react.”
  • “Recover your base.”
  • “Choose the next action.”
  • “Control the whole exchange.”

Common Mistakes

  • Reacting automatically without reading position
  • Recovering into poor balance or alignment
  • Forcing the same response regardless of range
  • Rushing the return instead of maintaining control

Drill Ideas

  • Coach-directed recognition and response rounds
  • Controlled variable-response drills
  • Defense, recovery, and decision-making rounds
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
