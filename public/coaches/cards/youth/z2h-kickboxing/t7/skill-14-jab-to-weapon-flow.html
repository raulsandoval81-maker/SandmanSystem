Skill 14 — Jab to Weapon Flow

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Use the jab to establish range and reaction

B — Connect the appropriate kick, knee, or angle

C — Recover behind guard

Coaching Cues

  • “Fast out, fast back.”
  • “Own the distance.”
  • “Touch and move.”
  • “Jab sets everything up.”

Common Mistakes

  • Reaching with the chin
  • Dropping rear hand
  • Standing still after jabbing
  • Pushing the punch

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-only spar rounds
  • Jab and angle drills
  • Pad jab timing rounds
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
