Skill 10 — Balance Under Fatigue

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Preserve stance and breathing as fatigue rises

B — Choose simple reliable technique

C — Finish the round under control

Coaching Cues

  • “Stay sharp when tired.”
  • “Breathe and work.”
  • “Finish clean.”
  • “Effort with form.”

Common Mistakes

  • Quitting posture under fatigue
  • Dropping hands when tired
  • Rushing instead of working clean
  • Letting effort erase technique

Drill Ideas

  • Muay Thai conditioning rounds
  • Bag sprint intervals
  • Technique-under-fatigue drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
