Skill 12 — Defensive Posture

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Select guard and frames for the current range

B — Protect while seeing the opponent clearly

C — Return to a stable defensive base

Coaching Cues

  • “Hands come home.”
  • “Protect while attacking.”
  • “Guard stays disciplined.”
  • “See through your defense.”

Common Mistakes

  • Dropping hands during combinations
  • Opening elbows too wide
  • Pulling chin upward
  • Forgetting defense after kicks

Drill Ideas

  • Guard recovery rounds
  • Defense-only movement drills
  • Partner touch-defense rounds
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
