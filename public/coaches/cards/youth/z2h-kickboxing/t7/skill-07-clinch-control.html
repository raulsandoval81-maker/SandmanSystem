Skill 7 — Clinch Control

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Establish posture, frames, and inside hand position

B — Turn or stabilize the partner without pulling the neck

C — Release or add a controlled knee only on the coach cue

Coaching Cues

  • “Head up.”
  • “Strong posture.”
  • “Control inside space.”
  • “Don’t hang.”

Common Mistakes

  • Bending at the waist
  • Loose hand control
  • Standing square
  • Losing balance during contact

Drill Ideas

  • Clinch position rounds
  • Pummel and posture drills
  • Clinch-to-knee entries
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
