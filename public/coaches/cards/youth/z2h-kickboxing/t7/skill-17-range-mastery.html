Skill 17 — Range Mastery

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Recognize kicking, punching, knee, and clinch ranges

B — Dictate the range with feet, guard, and teep

C — Transition on purpose rather than drifting

Coaching Cues

  • “Own the range.”
  • “Don’t crowd yourself.”
  • “Enter clean, exit clean.”
  • “See the space.”

Common Mistakes

  • Standing too close while striking
  • Backing straight up under pressure
  • Reaching instead of stepping
  • Losing stance during exits

Drill Ideas

  • Partner range drills
  • Distance touch rounds
  • Entry-and-exit movement drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
