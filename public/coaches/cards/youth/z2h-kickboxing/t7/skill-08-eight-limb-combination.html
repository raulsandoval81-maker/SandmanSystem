Skill 8 — Eight-Limb Combination

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Connect punches and kicks across safe ranges

B — Add a controlled knee or elbow pad when assigned

C — Finish balanced with guard and awareness

Coaching Cues

  • “Everything connects.”
  • “Stay balanced.”
  • “Hands return home.”
  • “Flow, don’t rush.”

Common Mistakes

  • Throwing too fast without control
  • Dropping hands during combinations
  • Losing stance between strikes
  • Stopping movement after attacks

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-cross-hook rounds
  • Punch-to-kick combinations
  • Pad combination drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
