Skill 6 — Teep System

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Use the teep to manage range and rhythm

B — Recover balance for the next weapon

C — Select a safe follow-up from the reaction

Coaching Cues

  • “Push through the target.”
  • “Balance first.”
  • “Hands stay active.”
  • “Reset fast.”

Common Mistakes

  • Leaning backward excessively
  • Dropping hands during the kick
  • Pushing without hip engagement
  • Falling out of stance after contact

Drill Ideas

  • Teep distance drills
  • Partner push-kick rounds
  • Teep and reset movement drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
