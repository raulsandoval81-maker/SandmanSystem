Skill 15 — Cross to Kick Flow

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Create a clean cross line

B — Use the rotation to load a balanced kick

C — Recover stance before continuing

Coaching Cues

  • “Turn the hip.”
  • “Straight line power.”
  • “Hands recover.”
  • “Don’t fall forward.”

Common Mistakes

  • Overreaching the punch
  • Dropping lead hand
  • Losing balance after impact
  • Arm punching without rotation

Drill Ideas

  • Cross mechanics rounds
  • Jab-cross pad drills
  • Cross and angle exits
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
