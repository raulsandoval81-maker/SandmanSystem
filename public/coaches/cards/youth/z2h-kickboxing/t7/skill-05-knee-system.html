Skill 5 — Knee System

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Establish posture and safe clinch or pad position

B — Deliver controlled straight and turning knees with balance

C — Return to stance or stable clinch control

Coaching Cues

  • “Drive the hip.”
  • “Stay tall.”
  • “Hands active.”
  • “Reset after contact.”

Common Mistakes

  • Leaning backward excessively
  • Dropping hands
  • Weak hip engagement
  • Falling forward after the strike

Drill Ideas

  • Knee strike repetitions
  • Pad knee rounds
  • Clinch-to-knee drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
