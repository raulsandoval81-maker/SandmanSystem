Skill 9 — Muay Thai Work Capacity

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T7 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Maintain technical posture through the work interval

B — Blend weapons without sacrificing balance

C — Recover breathing while staying composed

Coaching Cues

  • “Hit clean.”
  • “Don’t chase the bag.”
  • “Hands come home.”
  • “Reset after every combo.”

Common Mistakes

  • Standing too close to the bag
  • Pushing punches instead of striking
  • Dropping hands during combinations
  • Losing balance after kicks

Drill Ideas

  • Timed heavy bag rounds
  • One-combo reset rounds
  • Punch-kick bag flow drills
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
