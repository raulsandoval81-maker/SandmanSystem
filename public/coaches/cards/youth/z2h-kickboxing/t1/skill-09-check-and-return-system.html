Skill 9 — Check and Return System

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T1 — Prospect

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read and check the kick with posture

B — Replace the checking foot in stance

C — Return safely before the opponent resets

Coaching Cues

  • “Knee up fast.”
  • “Hands stay disciplined.”
  • “Defend and recover.”
  • “Stay balanced.”

Common Mistakes

  • Dropping hands during checks
  • Turning sideways too far
  • Losing balance after defense
  • Checking too low

Drill Ideas

  • Kick-and-check rounds
  • Partner reaction drills
  • Defend-and-return combinations
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
