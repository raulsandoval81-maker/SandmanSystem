Skill 6 — Eight-Limb Integration

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T6 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Select punches, kicks, knees, or controlled elbows for the range

B — Transition without losing posture or balance

C — Finish protected and ready to defend, clinch, or exit

Coaching Cues

  • “Choose the limb for the range.”
  • “Posture connects everything.”
  • “Recover before the next weapon.”
  • “Control every transition.”

Common Mistakes

  • Forcing a strike that does not fit the range
  • Dropping the guard between weapons
  • Losing balance during range transitions
  • Using elbows without the assigned pad and contact rules

Drill Ideas

  • Four-range pad selection rounds
  • Punch-to-kick-to-knee flow drills
  • Controlled elbow-pad integration
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
