Skill 19 — Corner Dictation

Journey: Road2Champion™ — Muay Thai

Tier: Youth T6 — Champion

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Guide the opponent toward the corner with balanced pressure

B — Read and close the safest escape without crossing the feet

C — Score with controlled strikes or clinch position, then reset

Coaching Cues

  • “Guide, don’t chase.”
  • “Own the exit.”
  • “Stay balanced while scoring.”

Common Mistakes

  • Following instead of cutting the ring
  • Smothering the attack
  • Becoming reckless at the boundary

Drill Ideas

  • Corner steering footwork
  • Escape-choice pad rounds
  • Controlled corner position rounds
  • All partner work follows coach-assigned contact and safety rules
