Skill 12 — Body Shot System

Tree: Punch Systems

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Expose The Body

B — Attack With Proper Level Change

C — Return Upstairs Or Exit

Coaching Cues

  • “Earn the body shot”
  • “Change level with the legs”
  • “Body work builds future openings”

Common Mistakes

  • Diving toward the target
  • Looking down during the attack
  • Throwing body shots without a safe entry

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-body-cross system
  • Body-hook angle drill
  • Body attack accumulation rounds

Progression

  • Use body attacks to weaken guard, legs, and rhythm
