Skill 4 — Range Dictation

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Establish Preferred Range

B — Keep The Opponent At That Range

C — Change Range On Your Terms

Coaching Cues

  • “Make them fight at your distance”
  • “Feet set the range”
  • “Enter and leave by choice”

Common Mistakes

  • Allowing the opponent to choose distance
  • Leaning instead of stepping
  • Remaining in the pocket after the attack

Drill Ideas

  • Long-range jab rounds
  • Range-change callout drill
  • Partner distance-control game

Progression

  • Connect range dictation to counter pressure
