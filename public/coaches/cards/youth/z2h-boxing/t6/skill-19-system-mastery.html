Skill 19 — System Mastery

Tree: Mastery

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Demonstrate Each Core Boxing System

B — Connect Systems Under Pressure

C — Select The Right System Independently

Coaching Cues

  • “Know the system, then make it yours”
  • “Connect skill, position, and decision”
  • “Mastery means correct choice under pressure”

Common Mistakes

  • Performing skills only in isolation
  • Depending on coach commands for every decision
  • Abandoning fundamentals during live work

Drill Ideas

  • Multi-system skill circuit
  • Unscripted tactical rounds
  • Teach-back and demonstration drill

Progression

  • Demonstrate Commander-level ownership before the final standard
