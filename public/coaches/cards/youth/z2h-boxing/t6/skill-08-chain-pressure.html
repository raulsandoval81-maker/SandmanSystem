Skill 8 — Chain Pressure

Tree: Pressure Boxing

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Begin With Safe Forward Pressure

B — Link Attacks As The Opponent Moves

C — Reposition And Continue

Coaching Cues

  • “Every reaction creates the next attack”
  • “Move with the escape”
  • “Pressure in layers”

Common Mistakes

  • Stopping after the first combination
  • Throwing while the feet are disconnected
  • Losing control while following movement

Drill Ideas

  • Combination-cutoff-combination drill
  • Moving heavy-bag pressure rounds
  • Partner chain-pressure rounds

Progression

  • Connect chain pressure to complete punch systems
