Skill 3 — Pressure Control

Tree: Pressure Boxing

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Take Space Safely

B — Maintain Controlled Pressure

C — Prevent The Easy Escape

Coaching Cues

  • “Pressure is position, not rushing”
  • “Stay behind the jab”
  • “Make them work without losing balance”

Common Mistakes

  • Chasing instead of cutting the ring
  • Walking into counters
  • Smothering punches at close range

Drill Ideas

  • Pressure without punching drill
  • Jab-and-cutoff rounds
  • Partner escape-control rounds

Progression

  • Control pressure before adding finishing combinations
