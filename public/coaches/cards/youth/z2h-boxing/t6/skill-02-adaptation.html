Skill 2 — Adaptation

Tree: Fight IQ

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Identify What Is Failing

B — Change The Approach

C — Test The New Solution

Coaching Cues

  • “Do not stay stuck”
  • “Change the answer, not the effort”
  • “Solve the problem in front of you”

Common Mistakes

  • Repeating the same unsuccessful pattern
  • Changing everything at once
  • Panicking when the first plan fails

Drill Ideas

  • Mid-round rule change
  • Opponent-style switch drill
  • Coach-directed adaptation rounds

Progression

  • Adapt without losing stance, discipline, or identity
