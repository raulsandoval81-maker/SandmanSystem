Skill 11 — Hook System

Tree: Punch Systems

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Create Hook Range

B — Select Lead Or Rear Hook

C — Continue Through A Safe Angle

Coaching Cues

  • “Short, tight, and balanced”
  • “Turn around the stance”
  • “The hook connects levels and angles”

Common Mistakes

  • Swinging outside the frame
  • Loading the hook visibly
  • Remaining square after the punch

Drill Ideas

  • Lead-rear hook flow
  • Body-head hook system
  • Hook-and-pivot rounds

Progression

  • Blend hook variations into complete combinations
