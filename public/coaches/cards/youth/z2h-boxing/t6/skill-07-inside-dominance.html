Skill 7 — Inside Dominance

Tree: Inside Boxing

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Win Head And Hand Position

B — Work With Compact Punches

C — Turn Or Exit On Advantage

Coaching Cues

  • “Position before punches”
  • “Short weapons inside”
  • “Stay strong through the hips”

Common Mistakes

  • Swinging long punches at close range
  • Standing upright in the pocket
  • Accepting poor head position

Drill Ideas

  • Shoulder-position partner drill
  • Short-hook and uppercut rounds
  • Inside turn-and-exit drill

Progression

  • Use inside dominance to build chain pressure
