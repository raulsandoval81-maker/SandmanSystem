Skill 17 — Fight Breaking

Tree: Pressure Boxing

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Identify The Opponent’s Strongest Pattern

B — Repeatedly Deny Or Punish It

C — Force The Opponent Into A Weaker Fight

Coaching Cues

  • “Break the pattern, not the rules”
  • “Take away what they trust”
  • “Make them fight your fight”

Common Mistakes

  • Trying to overpower every exchange
  • Ignoring the opponent’s best weapon
  • Changing tactics before the pressure takes effect

Drill Ideas

  • Weapon-removal sparring
  • Pattern-denial rounds
  • Coach-assigned breaking strategy

Progression

  • Use controlled pressure to dismantle an opponent’s confidence and rhythm
