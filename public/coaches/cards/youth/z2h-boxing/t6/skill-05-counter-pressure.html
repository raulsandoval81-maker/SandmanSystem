Skill 5 — Counter Pressure

Tree: Counter Boxing

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Invite The Forward Attack

B — Interrupt The Entry

C — Reclaim Position

Coaching Cues

  • “Make pressure pay”
  • “Meet them before they settle”
  • “Counter, then take the space back”

Common Mistakes

  • Retreating endlessly
  • Countering while off balance
  • Failing to regain ring position

Drill Ideas

  • Pressure-entry counter drill
  • Jab-and-pivot counter round
  • Rope counter-and-exit drill

Progression

  • Use counter pressure to discourage reckless entries
