Skill 6 — Corner Traps

Tree: Pressure Boxing

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Guide The Opponent Toward The Corner

B — Block The Best Exit

C — Attack While Remaining Balanced

Coaching Cues

  • “Close the door before punching”
  • “Stand where the escape wants to go”
  • “Do not fall into the corner”

Common Mistakes

  • Following directly behind the opponent
  • Attacking before the exit is controlled
  • Standing too square in front of the corner

Drill Ideas

  • Corner-guiding footwork drill
  • One-exit partner game
  • Corner attack-and-reset rounds

Progression

  • Connect corner traps to inside dominance
