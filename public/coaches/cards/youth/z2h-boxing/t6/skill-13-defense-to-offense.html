Skill 13 — Defense To Offense

Tree: Defense

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Complete The Defense

B — Recognize The Counter Window

C — Respond Before The Opponent Resets

Coaching Cues

  • “Defense is the beginning”
  • “Make them miss and make them answer”
  • “Counter from balance”

Common Mistakes

  • Defending without returning
  • Countering before the defense is complete
  • Using a counter that does not match the opening

Drill Ideas

  • Block-counter selection drill
  • Slip-to-combination drill
  • Random defense-to-offense rounds

Progression

  • Turn defensive success into immediate control
