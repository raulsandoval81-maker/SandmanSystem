Skill 1 — Boxing IQ

Tree: Fight IQ

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read The Situation

B — Choose The Best Response

C — Adjust After The Exchange

Coaching Cues

  • “Think while you box”
  • “Read before you react”
  • “Every exchange gives information”

Common Mistakes

  • Throwing without a purpose
  • Repeating failed attacks
  • Ignoring the opponent’s reactions

Drill Ideas

  • Coach scenario rounds
  • Pause-and-explain sparring
  • One-minute tactical problem rounds

Progression

  • Use boxing IQ to make real-time adaptations
