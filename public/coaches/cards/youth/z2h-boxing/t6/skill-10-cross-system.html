Skill 10 — Cross System

Tree: Punch Systems

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Create A Clear Line

B — Deliver The Cross With Structure

C — Continue, Angle, Or Exit

Coaching Cues

  • “Set the cross up”
  • “Drive from the floor”
  • “Never admire the punch”

Common Mistakes

  • Leading with an obvious cross
  • Falling over the front leg
  • Failing to recover the rear side

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-cross setup variations
  • Slip-cross and parry-cross drill
  • Cross-angle-exit rounds

Progression

  • Connect the cross system to hooks and body attacks
