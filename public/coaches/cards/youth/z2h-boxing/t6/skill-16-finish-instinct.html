Skill 16 — Finish Instinct

Tree: Finishing

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Recognize A Hurt Or Trapped Opponent

B — Increase Pressure With Control

C — Finish The Exchange Without Becoming Reckless

Coaching Cues

  • “Recognize the moment”
  • “Stay sharp when they weaken”
  • “Controlled urgency”

Common Mistakes

  • Rushing wildly after landing clean
  • Forgetting defense during the finish
  • Missing the opportunity by hesitating

Drill Ideas

  • Coach-called finish rounds
  • Hurt-opponent scenario drill
  • Three-second controlled burst drill

Progression

  • Develop finishing instinct without sacrificing discipline
