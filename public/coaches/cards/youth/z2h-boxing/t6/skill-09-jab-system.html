Skill 9 — Jab System

Tree: Punch Systems

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Select The Jab Variation

B — Create The Desired Reaction

C — Build The Next Attack

Coaching Cues

  • “The jab is more than one punch”
  • “Change speed, height, and purpose”
  • “Use the reaction you create”

Common Mistakes

  • Throwing every jab identically
  • Jabbing without watching the response
  • Failing to connect the jab to another weapon

Drill Ideas

  • Five-jab variation round
  • Jab reaction partner drill
  • Jab-to-combination pads

Progression

  • Build a reliable jab system for different opponents
