Skill 15 — Opponent Reading

Tree: Fight IQ

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Observe Repeated Behaviors

B — Test The Suspected Pattern

C — Exploit The Confirmed Habit

Coaching Cues

  • “Watch what happens twice”
  • “Test before you trust the read”
  • “Patterns reveal openings”

Common Mistakes

  • Assuming after one exchange
  • Watching only the hands
  • Missing reactions caused by feints

Drill Ideas

  • Habit-identification rounds
  • Feint-and-read drill
  • Coach-question sparring

Progression

  • Connect opponent reading to finishing opportunities
