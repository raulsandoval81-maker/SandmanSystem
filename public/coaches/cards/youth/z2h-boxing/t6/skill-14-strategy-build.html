Skill 14 — Strategy Build

Tree: Fight IQ

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Identify The Opponent’s Habits

B — Choose A Primary Plan

C — Prepare A Secondary Answer

Coaching Cues

  • “Build around what you see”
  • “Have a reason for the plan”
  • “Prepare the next answer early”

Common Mistakes

  • Building a plan without observing
  • Using too many goals at once
  • Refusing to adjust the strategy

Drill Ideas

  • Opponent profile worksheet round
  • One-plan sparring round
  • Plan A and Plan B scenario drill

Progression

  • Build simple strategies that can survive live pressure
