Skill 18 — Weapon Blending

Tree: Fight IQ

Tier: Youth T6 — Commander

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Begin With One Reliable Weapon

B — Add A Complementary Weapon

C — Flow Between Them Based On Reaction

Coaching Cues

  • “Weapons should support each other”
  • “Blend levels, sides, and ranges”
  • “Let the reaction choose the next punch”

Common Mistakes

  • Combining punches without tactical connection
  • Using too many weapons at once
  • Forcing a preset sequence despite the reaction

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-hook blend drill
  • Head-body weapon rounds
  • Open-choice combination pads

Progression

  • Blend punch systems into an adaptable personal style
