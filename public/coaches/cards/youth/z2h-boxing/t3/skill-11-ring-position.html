Skill 11 — Ring Position

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Recognize The Position

B — Move Toward Advantage

C — Avoid Being Trapped

Coaching Cues

  • “Know where you are”
  • “Own the center”
  • “Do not follow blindly”

Common Mistakes

  • Drifting toward the ropes
  • Chasing instead of cutting space
  • Ignoring the corners

Drill Ideas

  • Center-ring control drill
  • Rope awareness drill
  • Cone position game

Progression

  • Connect ring position to ring awareness
