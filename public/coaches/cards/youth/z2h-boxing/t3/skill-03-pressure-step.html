Skill 3 — Pressure Step

Tree: Pressure Boxing

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Close Space Safely

B — Maintain The Guard

C — Hold Strong Position

Coaching Cues

  • “Take space, do not chase”
  • “Stay behind your hands”
  • “Balanced pressure”

Common Mistakes

  • Leaning forward
  • Crossing the feet
  • Walking into punches

Drill Ideas

  • Guarded pressure walk
  • Partner retreat-and-follow drill
  • Jab pressure step

Progression

  • Connect pressure steps to pressure flow
