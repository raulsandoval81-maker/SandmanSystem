Skill 7 — Angle Attack

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Create The Angle

B — Attack From The New Position

C — Recover Facing The Opponent

Coaching Cues

  • “Move before they reset”
  • “Attack from the side”
  • “Always face the opponent”

Common Mistakes

  • Crossing the feet
  • Turning the back
  • Creating an angle without attacking

Drill Ideas

  • Pivot-and-punch drill
  • Cone angle attack
  • Partner angle-entry drill

Progression

  • Connect angle attacks to ring position
