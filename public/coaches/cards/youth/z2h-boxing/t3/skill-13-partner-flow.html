Skill 13 — Partner Flow

Tree: Applied Boxing

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Give A Clear Action

B — Receive And Respond

C — Continue With Control

Coaching Cues

  • “Work together”
  • “Give your partner a good look”
  • “Smooth and controlled”

Common Mistakes

  • Trying to win the drill
  • Increasing power without permission
  • Breaking the assigned pattern

Drill Ideas

  • Attack-defense flow
  • Combination-response flow
  • Continuous partner movement

Progression

  • Connect partner flow to controlled sparring
