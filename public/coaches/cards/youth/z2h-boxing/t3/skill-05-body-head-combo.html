Skill 5 — Body Head Combo

Tree: Combinations

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Change Level

B — Attack The Body

C — Return To The Head

Coaching Cues

  • “Bend with the knees”
  • “Bring the eyes back up”
  • “Body opens the head”

Common Mistakes

  • Bending at the waist
  • Looking down during the body shot
  • Standing still after the combination

Drill Ideas

  • Body-head pad drill
  • Bag level-change drill
  • Partner body-head targets

Progression

  • Connect body-head attacks to combination flow
