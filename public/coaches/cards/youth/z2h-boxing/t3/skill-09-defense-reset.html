Skill 9 — Defense Reset

Tree: Defense

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Complete The Defense

B — Rebuild Stance And Guard

C — Re-enter The Exchange

Coaching Cues

  • “Defend, reset, return”
  • “Hands and feet rebuild together”
  • “Do not stay folded”

Common Mistakes

  • Backing away without stance
  • Keeping the hands out of position
  • Waiting too long to re-engage

Drill Ideas

  • Shell-reset-counter drill
  • Slip-reset-jab drill
  • Partner defense reset rounds

Progression

  • Connect defense reset to offense
