Skill 2 — Combination Entries

Tree: Entries

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Create The Opening

B — Enter With A Combination

C — Exit Or Reposition

Coaching Cues

  • “Enter with purpose”
  • “Hands and feet arrive together”
  • “Do not stay in front”

Common Mistakes

  • Entering from too far away
  • Punching before the feet are set
  • Remaining stationary after the combination

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-cross entry
  • Double-jab entry
  • Combination-entry partner drill

Progression

  • Connect combination entries to pressure stepping
