Skill 4 — Three Punch Flow

Tree: Combinations

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Begin With A Clean Entry

B — Flow Through Three Punches

C — Finish Balanced

Coaching Cues

  • “Smooth, not rushed”
  • “Every punch returns home”
  • “Finish in stance”

Common Mistakes

  • Pausing between punches
  • Throwing every punch at the same speed
  • Losing balance on the final punch

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-cross-hook flow
  • Pad three-punch combinations
  • Bag combination rounds

Progression

  • Connect three-punch flow to longer combinations
