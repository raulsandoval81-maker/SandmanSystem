Skill 14 — Controlled Sparring

Tree: Applied Boxing

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Follow The Round Rule

B — Apply Skills With Control

C — Protect The Partner

Coaching Cues

  • “Skill before power”
  • “Stay within the assignment”
  • “Make your partner better”

Common Mistakes

  • Turning the drill into a fight
  • Ignoring the assigned objective
  • Using excessive contact

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-only sparring
  • Body-only touch sparring
  • Attack-defense role rounds

Progression

  • Connect controlled sparring to live rounds
