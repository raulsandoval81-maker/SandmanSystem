Skill 1 — Jab Entry

Tree: Entries

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Establish Range

B — Enter Behind The Jab

C — Finish In Position

Coaching Cues

  • “The jab opens the door”
  • “Step with the punch”
  • “Enter behind your guard”

Common Mistakes

  • Reaching instead of stepping
  • Entering with the chin high
  • Stopping directly in front

Drill Ideas

  • Step-jab entry
  • Partner target entry
  • Jab-entry and exit drill

Progression

  • Connect the jab entry to combination entries
