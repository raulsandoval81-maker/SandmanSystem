Skill 6 — Combination Flow

Tree: Combinations

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Link The Punches

B — Change Levels And Rhythm

C — Exit Under Control

Coaching Cues

  • “Let one punch build the next”
  • “Change speed and level”
  • “Finish with defense”

Common Mistakes

  • Memorizing without reacting
  • Throwing every punch with maximum power
  • Forgetting defense at the end

Drill Ideas

  • Four-punch flow drill
  • Coach-called combinations
  • Bag flow rounds

Progression

  • Connect combination flow to angle attacks
