Skill 12 — Ring Awareness

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Track The Opponent

B — Track The Boundaries

C — Choose The Best Position

Coaching Cues

  • “See the whole ring”
  • “Know what is behind you”
  • “Move with purpose”

Common Mistakes

  • Watching only the opponent’s hands
  • Backing into corners
  • Moving without a positional goal

Drill Ideas

  • Boundary callout drill
  • Partner ring-position game
  • Eyes-up movement rounds

Progression

  • Connect ring awareness to ringcraft
