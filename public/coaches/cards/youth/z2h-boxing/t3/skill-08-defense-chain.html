Skill 8 — Defense Chain

Tree: Defense

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Defend The First Attack

B — Flow Into A Second Defense

C — Recover Or Counter

Coaching Cues

  • “One defense leads to the next”
  • “Stay calm and compact”
  • “Finish ready to answer”

Common Mistakes

  • Freezing after the first defense
  • Making movements too large
  • Losing sight of the opponent

Drill Ideas

  • Parry-slip chain
  • Block-roll chain
  • Partner two-attack defense drill

Progression

  • Connect defense chains to defensive reactions
