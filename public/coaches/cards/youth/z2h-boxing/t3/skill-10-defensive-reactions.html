Skill 10 — Defensive Reactions

Tree: Timing & Reactions

Tier: Youth T3 — Competitor

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read The Attack

B — Select The Correct Defense

C — Recover Immediately

Coaching Cues

  • “Read, choose, respond”
  • “Do not guess”
  • “Stay under control”

Common Mistakes

  • Using the same defense every time
  • Reacting before the attack is shown
  • Losing balance during the response

Drill Ideas

  • Coach attack callout
  • Partner random-feed defense
  • Block-slip-parry reaction drill

Progression

  • Connect defensive reactions to controlled sparring
