Skill 4 — Jab

Tree: Straight Punches

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Lead Hand Fires

B — Shoulder Protects Chin

C — Hand Returns Home

Coaching Cues

  • “Straight out, straight back”
  • “Turn the fist over”
  • “Return to guard”

Common Mistakes

  • Dropping the hand before punching
  • Reaching too far
  • Leaving the jab extended

Drill Ideas

  • Wall-line jab
  • Mirror jab repetitions
  • Partner target jab

Progression

  • Connect jab to the cross
