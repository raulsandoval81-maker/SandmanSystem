Skill 2 — Guard

Tree: Stance & Motion

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Hands High

B — Elbows In

C — Chin Protected

Coaching Cues

  • “Hands home”
  • “Elbows protect the body”
  • “Look through your gloves”

Common Mistakes

  • Hands dropping after punches
  • Elbows flaring outward
  • Gloves blocking vision

Drill Ideas

  • Guard hold
  • Coach touch check
  • Punch-and-return drill

Progression

  • Connect guard to defense shell
