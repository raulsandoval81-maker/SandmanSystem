Skill 6 — One-Two

Tree: Straight Punches

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Jab Starts

B — Cross Follows

C — Guard Resets

Coaching Cues

  • “One, two, home”
  • “Punch down the middle”
  • “Finish balanced”

Common Mistakes

  • Pausing too long between punches
  • Crossing the feet
  • Finishing with hands down

Drill Ideas

  • Shadow one-two
  • Focus-target one-two
  • Step-in one-two

Progression

  • Connect the one-two to combination work
