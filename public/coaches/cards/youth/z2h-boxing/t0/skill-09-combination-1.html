Skill 9 — Combination 1

Tree: Combinations

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Enter With Jab

B — Add The Cross

C — Exit In Guard

Coaching Cues

  • “Enter, score, leave”
  • “Hands return home”
  • “Stay in stance”

Common Mistakes

  • Rushing the punches
  • Standing still after the combination
  • Losing balance on the cross

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-cross-exit shadow drill
  • Partner target combination
  • Combination and reset drill

Progression

  • Connect Combination 1 to angle exits
