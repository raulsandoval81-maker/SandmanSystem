Skill 10 — Heavy Bag

Tree: Applied Boxing

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Set The Stance

B — Strike With Control

C — Move After Punching

Coaching Cues

  • “Do not push the bag”
  • “Punch and return”
  • “Move around your work”

Common Mistakes

  • Standing directly in front of the bag
  • Swinging only with the arms
  • Losing guard between punches

Drill Ideas

  • Single-punch rounds
  • One-two and move
  • Coach-command bag round

Progression

  • Connect bag work to controlled rounds
