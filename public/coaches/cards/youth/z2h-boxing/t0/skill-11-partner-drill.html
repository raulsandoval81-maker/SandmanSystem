Skill 11 — Partner Drill

Tree: Applied Boxing

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Partner Gives A Clear Target

B — Boxer Strikes With Control

C — Both Athletes Reset

Coaching Cues

  • “Help your partner learn”
  • “Control every punch”
  • “Reset together”

Common Mistakes

  • Hitting too hard
  • Targets moving unpredictably
  • Failing to reset between repetitions

Drill Ideas

  • Jab target exchange
  • One-two target exchange
  • Attack-and-shell partner drill

Progression

  • Connect partner drilling to controlled sparring
