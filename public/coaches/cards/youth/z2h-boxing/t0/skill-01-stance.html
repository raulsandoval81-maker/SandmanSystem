Skill 1 — Stance

Tree: Stance & Motion

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Feet Set

B — Knees Bent

C — Hands Ready

Coaching Cues

  • “Strong base”
  • “Chin down”
  • “Hands home”

Common Mistakes

  • Feet too close together
  • Standing too tall
  • Chin lifted

Drill Ideas

  • Stance hold
  • Coach position check
  • Mirror stance drill

Progression

  • Connect stance to footwork
