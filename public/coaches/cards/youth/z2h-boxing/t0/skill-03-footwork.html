Skill 3 — Footwork

Tree: Stance & Motion

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Step With Direction

B — Rear Foot Follows

C — Stance Stays Set

Coaching Cues

  • “Step, then slide”
  • “Do not cross your feet”
  • “Stay balanced”

Common Mistakes

  • Feet crossing
  • Feet coming together
  • Leaning while moving

Drill Ideas

  • Line-step drill
  • Forward-and-back movement
  • Coach direction callout

Progression

  • Connect footwork to distance control
