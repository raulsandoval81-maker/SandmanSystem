Skill 5 — Cross

Tree: Straight Punches

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Rear Hand Fires

B — Hip And Shoulder Turn

C — Rear Hand Returns

Coaching Cues

  • “Turn the hip”
  • “Rear heel turns”
  • “Bring the hand home”

Common Mistakes

  • Arm punching without rotation
  • Overturning the body
  • Dropping the lead hand

Drill Ideas

  • Hip-turn drill
  • Mirror cross repetitions
  • Partner target cross

Progression

  • Connect cross to the one-two
