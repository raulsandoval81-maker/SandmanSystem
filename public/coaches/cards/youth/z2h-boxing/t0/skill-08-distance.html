Skill 8 — Distance

Tree: Ring Awareness

Tier: Youth T0 — Shadow

Structure (A/B/C)

A — See The Range

B — Step Into Range

C — Exit Safely

Coaching Cues

  • “Too far, safe, or close”
  • “Touch without reaching”
  • “Move your feet first”

Common Mistakes

  • Reaching with punches
  • Standing too close
  • Backing straight up with hands down

Drill Ideas

  • Glove-touch range drill
  • Step-in and step-out drill
  • Coach distance callout

Progression

  • Connect distance to combination entry
