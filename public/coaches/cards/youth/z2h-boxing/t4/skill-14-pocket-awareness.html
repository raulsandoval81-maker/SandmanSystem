Skill 14 — Pocket Awareness

Tree: Inside Boxing

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Recognize Close Range

B — Stay Compact And Protected

C — Punch, Turn, Or Exit

Coaching Cues

  • “Know when you are in the pocket”
  • “Short punches, tight guard”
  • “Do not stay still inside”

Common Mistakes

  • Throwing long punches at close range
  • Standing tall in the pocket
  • Losing awareness of exit angles

Drill Ideas

  • Pocket-position partner drill
  • Short-combination rounds
  • Punch-and-pivot pocket drill

Progression

  • Connect pocket awareness to inside dominance
