Skill 8 — Body Attack

Tree: Body Boxing

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Create The Opening

B — Change Level Safely

C — Return To The Head Or Exit

Coaching Cues

  • “Eyes stay up”
  • “Bend with the knees”
  • “Body work creates openings”

Common Mistakes

  • Dropping both hands
  • Bending at the waist
  • Remaining low after the punch

Drill Ideas

  • Body-head pad drill
  • Double-body bag drill
  • Partner body-target rounds

Progression

  • Connect body attack to body-shot systems
