Skill 10 — Combination Defense

Tree: Defense

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Defend The First Punch

B — Stay Calm Through The Combination

C — Exit Or Counter

Coaching Cues

  • “Do not freeze after one defense”
  • “Stay compact”
  • “Finish in position”

Common Mistakes

  • Turning away
  • Opening the guard between punches
  • Backing straight up without balance

Drill Ideas

  • Three-punch defense drill
  • Shell-slip-roll sequence
  • Partner combination-defense rounds

Progression

  • Connect combination defense to defensive layers
