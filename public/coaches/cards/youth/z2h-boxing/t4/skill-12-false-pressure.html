Skill 12 — False Pressure

Tree: Tactical Boxing

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Show Forward Pressure

B — Create The Opponent’s Reaction

C — Counter Or Reposition

Coaching Cues

  • “Make them think you are coming”
  • “Draw the reaction”
  • “Stay ready to answer”

Common Mistakes

  • Committing too deeply
  • Feinting without watching the reaction
  • Standing still after drawing the response

Drill Ideas

  • Step-feint counter drill
  • False-entry partner drill
  • Pressure-and-pullback rounds

Progression

  • Connect false pressure to counter pressure
