Skill 6 — Advanced Jab

Tree: Punch Systems

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Change The Jab Look

B — Control Range And Rhythm

C — Build The Next Attack

Coaching Cues

  • “Different jabs, same strong stance”
  • “Change speed and level”
  • “Use the jab to create”

Common Mistakes

  • Throwing every jab the same way
  • Reaching from outside range
  • Failing to build off the jab

Drill Ideas

  • Double-and-single jab drill
  • Body-head jab drill
  • Moving jab rounds

Progression

  • Connect the advanced jab to the jab system
