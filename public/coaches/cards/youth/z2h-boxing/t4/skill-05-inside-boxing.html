Skill 5 — Inside Boxing

Tree: Inside Boxing

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Enter Behind The Guard

B — Work From A Compact Position

C — Exit Safely

Coaching Cues

  • “Short punches inside”
  • “Keep the elbows tight”
  • “Head in a safe position”

Common Mistakes

  • Swinging wide punches
  • Standing upright at close range
  • Remaining inside without control

Drill Ideas

  • Short-punch bag drill
  • Partner inside-position drill
  • Body-hook inside flow

Progression

  • Connect inside boxing to pocket awareness
