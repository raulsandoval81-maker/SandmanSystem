Skill 9 — Head Movement

Tree: Defense

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read The Punch Line

B — Move Just Off Center

C — Recover Ready To Counter

Coaching Cues

  • “Small movement wins”
  • “Move from the knees”
  • “Make them miss, then answer”

Common Mistakes

  • Moving too far
  • Bending at the waist
  • Moving without maintaining stance

Drill Ideas

  • Slip-rope rounds
  • Partner jab-slip drill
  • Head-movement shadowboxing

Progression

  • Connect head movement to counter chains
