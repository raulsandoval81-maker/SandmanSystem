Skill 2 — Pressure Flow

Tree: Pressure Boxing

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Close Space Safely

B — Keep The Opponent Working

C — Maintain Strong Position

Coaching Cues

  • “Pressure without chasing”
  • “Stay behind your hands”
  • “Take the space they give you”

Common Mistakes

  • Walking straight into punches
  • Leaning over the front foot
  • Rushing without control

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-pressure rounds
  • Partner retreat-and-follow drill
  • Pressure-step bag rounds

Progression

  • Connect pressure flow to corner pressure
