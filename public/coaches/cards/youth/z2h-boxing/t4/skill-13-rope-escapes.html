Skill 13 — Rope Escapes

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Recognize The Boundary

B — Create Space Or An Angle

C — Exit Facing The Opponent

Coaching Cues

  • “Do not wait on the ropes”
  • “Create the exit first”
  • “Leave with your eyes up”

Common Mistakes

  • Backing straight into the ropes
  • Turning away during the escape
  • Crossing the feet

Drill Ideas

  • Rope pivot drill
  • Partner pressure escape
  • Block-and-exit rounds

Progression

  • Connect rope escapes to corner escapes
