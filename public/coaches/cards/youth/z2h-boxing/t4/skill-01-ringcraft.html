Skill 1 — Ringcraft

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read The Ring

B — Control The Position

C — Move With Purpose

Coaching Cues

  • “Know where you are”
  • “Own the center”
  • “Make every step matter”

Common Mistakes

  • Following the opponent without cutting space
  • Drifting toward the ropes
  • Moving without a plan

Drill Ideas

  • Center-control drill
  • Corner-escape drill
  • Ring-position partner game

Progression

  • Connect ringcraft to pressure flow
