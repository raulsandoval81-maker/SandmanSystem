Skill 7 — Advanced Cross

Tree: Punch Systems

Tier: Youth T4 — Warrior

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Create The Opening

B — Drive Through The Target

C — Recover Into Position

Coaching Cues

  • “Set the cross up”
  • “Turn the floor with the rear foot”
  • “Finish ready for the next punch”

Common Mistakes

  • Throwing the cross without a setup
  • Over-rotating
  • Falling over the lead foot

Drill Ideas

  • Jab-cross variation drill
  • Slip-cross drill
  • Cross-and-angle exit

Progression

  • Connect the advanced cross to the cross system
