Skill 2 — Rhythm

Tree: Timing & Reactions

Tier: Youth T2 — Contender

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Establish A Pace

B — Change The Beat

C — Attack On The Change

Coaching Cues

  • “Smooth, then sharp”
  • “Change the speed”
  • “Do not become predictable”

Common Mistakes

  • Moving at one speed
  • Pausing before every attack
  • Losing balance during tempo changes

Drill Ideas

  • Fast-slow shadowboxing
  • Rhythm jab drill
  • Coach tempo callout

Progression

  • Connect rhythm to combination flow
