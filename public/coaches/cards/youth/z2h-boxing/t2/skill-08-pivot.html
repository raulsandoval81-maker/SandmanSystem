Skill 8 — Pivot

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T2 — Contender

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Plant The Lead Foot

B — Turn Around The Lead Side

C — Reset The Stance

Coaching Cues

  • “Turn, do not cross”
  • “Stay over your feet”
  • “Finish ready”

Common Mistakes

  • Crossing the feet
  • Spinning too far
  • Standing tall during the turn

Drill Ideas

  • Cone pivot drill
  • Partner follow drill
  • Punch-and-pivot drill

Progression

  • Connect pivots to angle attacks
