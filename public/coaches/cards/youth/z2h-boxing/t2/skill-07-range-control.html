Skill 7 — Range Control

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T2 — Contender

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Identify The Range

B — Control With Feet And Jab

C — Exit Before The Return

Coaching Cues

  • “Make them reach”
  • “Own your space”
  • “Feet control distance”

Common Mistakes

  • Leaning instead of stepping
  • Remaining too close after punching
  • Backing straight up

Drill Ideas

  • Jab range drill
  • Step-in step-out drill
  • Partner distance game

Progression

  • Connect range control to ring position
