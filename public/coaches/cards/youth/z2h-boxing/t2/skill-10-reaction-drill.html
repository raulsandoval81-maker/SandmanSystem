Skill 10 — Reaction Drill

Tree: Timing & Reactions

Tier: Youth T2 — Contender

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read The Signal

B — Choose The Response

C — Recover Immediately

Coaching Cues

  • “Eyes first”
  • “Choose, do not guess”
  • “React under control”

Common Mistakes

  • Moving before the signal
  • Using the same response every time
  • Losing stance during reactions

Drill Ideas

  • Coach color callout
  • Partner attack-response drill
  • Defense-or-counter reaction drill

Progression

  • Connect reactions to defensive chains
