Skill 1 — Timing

Tree: Timing & Reactions

Tier: Youth T2 — Contender

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Watch The Opening

B — Punch At The Right Moment

C — Reset Safely

Coaching Cues

  • “Do not rush”
  • “See it, then go”
  • “Be first at the right time”

Common Mistakes

  • Punching without watching
  • Reacting too early
  • Waiting too long

Drill Ideas

  • Coach signal jab
  • Partner opening drill
  • Touch-and-react drill

Progression

  • Connect timing to counter punching
