Skill 9 — Exit Angle

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T2 — Contender

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Finish The Combination

B — Step Off The Center Line

C — Reset Facing The Opponent

Coaching Cues

  • “Do not stay in front”
  • “Punch, step, face”
  • “Leave in stance”

Common Mistakes

  • Backing straight out
  • Turning away
  • Crossing feet during the exit

Drill Ideas

  • One-two angle exit
  • Bag exit drill
  • Partner combination exit

Progression

  • Connect exit angles to ring position
