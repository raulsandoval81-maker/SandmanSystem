Skill 5 — Cross Counter

Tree: Counter Boxing

Tier: Youth T2 — Contender

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read The Lead Punch

B — Move Off The Line

C — Fire The Cross

Coaching Cues

  • “Move and answer”
  • “Turn the hip”
  • “Return to guard”

Common Mistakes

  • Standing directly in front
  • Throwing only with the arm
  • Dropping the lead hand

Drill Ideas

  • Parry-cross drill
  • Slip-cross drill
  • Partner jab feed

Progression

  • Connect the cross counter to follow-up punches
