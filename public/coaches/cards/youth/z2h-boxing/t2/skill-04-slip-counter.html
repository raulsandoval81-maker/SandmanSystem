Skill 4 — Slip Counter

Tree: Counter Boxing

Tier: Youth T2 — Contender

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Slip The Punch

B — Stay In Position

C — Return With A Counter

Coaching Cues

  • “Make them miss small”
  • “Eyes stay up”
  • “Defend, then answer”

Common Mistakes

  • Slipping too far
  • Bending at the waist
  • Waiting too long to counter

Drill Ideas

  • Slip-and-jab drill
  • Slip-and-cross drill
  • Partner feed counter drill

Progression

  • Connect slip counters into counter chains
