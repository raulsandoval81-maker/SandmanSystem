Skill 3 — Counter Jab

Tree: Counter Boxing

Tier: Youth T2 — Contender

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read The Entry

B — Fire The Jab

C — Exit Or Reset

Coaching Cues

  • “Beat them to the spot”
  • “Short and straight”
  • “Touch and move”

Common Mistakes

  • Reaching for the opponent
  • Dropping the rear hand
  • Standing still after the counter

Drill Ideas

  • Partner step-in counter jab
  • Coach target reaction
  • Jab-and-exit drill

Progression

  • Connect counter jab to combination counters
