Skill 7 — Hook Defense

Tree: Defense

Tier: Youth T1 — Recruit

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Guard Tight

B — Roll Or Block

C — Recover

Coaching Cues

  • “See the hook”
  • “Stay tight”
  • “Recover quickly”

Common Mistakes

  • Turning away
  • Hands low
  • Standing tall

Drill Ideas

  • Partner hook defense
  • Coach call drill
  • Mirror blocks

Progression

  • Connect defense to offense
