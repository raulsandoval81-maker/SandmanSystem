Skill 3 — Double Jab

Tree: Straight Punches

Tier: Youth T1 — Recruit

Structure (A/B/C)

A — First Jab

B — Second Jab

C — Stay Balanced

Coaching Cues

  • “Snap both jabs”
  • “Move your feet”
  • “Return to guard”

Common Mistakes

  • Pausing between jabs
  • Leaning forward
  • Dropping hands

Drill Ideas

  • Double jab shadow
  • Partner targets
  • Bag drill

Progression

  • Connect to body attack
