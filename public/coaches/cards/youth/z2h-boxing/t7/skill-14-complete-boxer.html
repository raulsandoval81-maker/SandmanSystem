Skill 14 — Complete Boxer

Tree: System Mastery

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Demonstrate Strength Across All Core Systems

B — Connect Systems According To The Situation

C — Maintain Identity While Adapting

Coaching Cues

  • “Complete does not mean complicated”
  • “Use the right system at the right time”
  • “Your style should still have structure”

Common Mistakes

  • Trying to display every skill in one exchange
  • Using advanced tools without foundational control
  • Changing style without tactical purpose

Drill Ideas

  • Complete-system circuit
  • Style-switch tactical rounds
  • Open-problem sparring

Progression

  • Show balanced ability in offense, defense, ringcraft, pressure, and strategy
