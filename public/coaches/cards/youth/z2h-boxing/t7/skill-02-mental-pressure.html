Skill 2 — Mental Pressure

Tree: Fight Psychology

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Establish A Reliable Threat

B — Repeat The Threat Until It Shapes Behavior

C — Exploit The Reaction It Creates

Coaching Cues

  • “Make them think about you”
  • “Consistency creates pressure”
  • “Control their choices before the punch lands”

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing aggression with mental pressure
  • Changing threats before they take effect
  • Ignoring the opponent’s emotional response

Drill Ideas

  • Repeated-threat sparring
  • Jab-pressure behavior drill
  • Feint-and-reaction tracking rounds

Progression

  • Use repeated technical threats to influence the opponent’s decisions
