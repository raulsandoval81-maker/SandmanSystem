Skill 15 — Leadership Through Boxing

Tree: Leadership

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Model Discipline And Control

B — Raise The Standard Of Training Partners

C — Teach Through Action And Communication

Coaching Cues

  • “Your example teaches before your words”
  • “Make the room better”
  • “Control is part of leadership”

Common Mistakes

  • Using skill to dominate weaker partners
  • Correcting others without modeling the standard
  • Confusing loudness with leadership

Drill Ideas

  • Partner mentorship rounds
  • Teach-back skill station
  • Lead-the-warmup assignment

Progression

  • Demonstrate that Hero-level boxing improves both self and team
