Skill 16 — Championship Rounds

Tree: Applied Boxing

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Enter With A Round Plan

B — Sustain Execution Through Fatigue

C — Finish With Technical And Mental Authority

Coaching Cues

  • “The final rounds reveal the standard”
  • “Do not borrow energy from technique”
  • “Finish with command”

Common Mistakes

  • Starting too fast without a plan
  • Letting fatigue remove tactical discipline
  • Surviving the final round instead of managing it

Drill Ideas

  • Extended technical rounds
  • Final-round scenario work
  • Fatigue-to-focus sparring

Progression

  • Perform at Hero standard when fatigue and pressure are both high
