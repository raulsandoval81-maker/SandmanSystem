Skill 9 — High Pressure Rounds

Tree: Applied Boxing

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Establish A Clear Round Objective

B — Maintain Technique At Elevated Intensity

C — Recover Without Losing Tactical Focus

Coaching Cues

  • “Intensity without panic”
  • “Keep the objective alive”
  • “High pressure still requires clean choices”

Common Mistakes

  • Turning the round into uncontrolled fighting
  • Ignoring the assigned tactical goal
  • Letting fatigue destroy stance

Drill Ideas

  • High-output technical rounds
  • Pressure-and-recovery intervals
  • Objective-based championship rounds

Progression

  • Perform technically and tactically while the pace remains demanding
