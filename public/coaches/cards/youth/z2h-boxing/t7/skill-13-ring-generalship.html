Skill 13 — Ring Generalship

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Establish Preferred Position And Pace

B — Direct The Opponent’s Movement

C — Control Where And How Exchanges Occur

Coaching Cues

  • “The ring should feel like yours”
  • “Lead the movement”
  • “Make the opponent respond to your terms”

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing movement with control
  • Giving away the center without purpose
  • Allowing the opponent to reset comfortably

Drill Ideas

  • Center-command rounds
  • Directional pressure game
  • Ring-control scoring rounds

Progression

  • Demonstrate visible command of position, tempo, and exchange location
