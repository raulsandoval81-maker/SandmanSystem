Skill 7 — Corner Dictation

Tree: Ringcraft

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Guide The Opponent Into The Corner

B — Dictate The Available Exit

C — Punish The Chosen Escape

Coaching Cues

  • “You decide where the door is”
  • “Stand where the escape wants to go”
  • “Control first, attack second”

Common Mistakes

  • Blocking every exit and smothering the attack
  • Following instead of guiding
  • Standing directly square in front

Drill Ideas

  • Single-exit corner game
  • Corner guide-and-counter drill
  • Controlled corner-pressure rounds

Progression

  • Dictate movement in the corner instead of merely trapping the opponent
