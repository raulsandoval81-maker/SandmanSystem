Skill 3 — Round Stealing

Tree: Fight Management

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read The State Of The Round

B — Increase Visible Control At The Right Moment

C — Finish The Round In Command

Coaching Cues

  • “Know when the round needs you”
  • “Finish stronger than you started”
  • “Make the last impression count”

Common Mistakes

  • Waiting too long to increase output
  • Throwing recklessly in the closing seconds
  • Mistaking activity for effective control

Drill Ideas

  • Thirty-second finish rounds
  • Coach score-call scenarios
  • Final-minute tactical surges

Progression

  • Learn to close competitive rounds with visible, intelligent control
