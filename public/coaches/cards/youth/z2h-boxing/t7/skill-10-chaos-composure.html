Skill 10 — Chaos Composure

Tree: Mental Mastery

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Control Breathing And Posture

B — Simplify The Immediate Decision

C — Rebuild Tactical Control

Coaching Cues

  • “Your calm is a weapon”
  • “Breathe, see, solve”
  • “Do not let disorder enter your mind”

Common Mistakes

  • Holding the breath under pressure
  • Trying to answer chaos with more chaos
  • Forgetting the simplest defensive option

Drill Ideas

  • Breathing-reset sparring
  • Bad-position recovery rounds
  • Coach-created chaos scenarios

Progression

  • Remain mentally organized when the exchange becomes physically disorganized
