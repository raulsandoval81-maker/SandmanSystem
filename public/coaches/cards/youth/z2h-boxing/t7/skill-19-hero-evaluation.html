Skill 19 — Hero Evaluation

Tree: Tier Evaluation

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Demonstrate Complete Technical Ownership

B — Solve Tactical Problems Independently

C — Display Leadership, Control, And Character

Coaching Cues

  • “Show who you have become”
  • “Solve without waiting for rescue”
  • “Technique, judgment, and character must agree”

Common Mistakes

  • Performing for appearance instead of execution
  • Waiting for constant coach direction
  • Losing composure when tested outside comfort

Drill Ideas

  • Comprehensive Hero skill circuit
  • Unscripted tactical evaluation
  • Leadership and partner-control assessment

Progression

  • Confirm readiness for the final Hero standard
