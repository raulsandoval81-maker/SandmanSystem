Skill 17 — Adaptive Strategy

Tree: Fight Strategy

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Begin With A Clear Plan

B — Measure The Opponent’s Response

C — Modify The Plan Without Losing The Objective

Coaching Cues

  • “Keep the goal, change the route”
  • “Adapt from evidence”
  • “A good plan can move”

Common Mistakes

  • Abandoning the plan after one failure
  • Refusing to change despite clear evidence
  • Adapting emotionally instead of logically

Drill Ideas

  • Plan-change sparring
  • Coach information rounds
  • Three-phase opponent scenarios

Progression

  • Adjust strategy intelligently while preserving the main objective
