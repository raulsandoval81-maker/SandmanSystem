Skill 4 — Live Chaos

Tree: Adaptability

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Stabilize Position Under Uncertainty

B — Identify The Immediate Problem

C — Apply The Simplest Reliable Solution

Coaching Cues

  • “Calm first, solution second”
  • “Return to what is reliable”
  • “Chaos does not remove your fundamentals”

Common Mistakes

  • Trying to solve every problem at once
  • Abandoning stance under pressure
  • Responding emotionally instead of tactically

Drill Ideas

  • Random-constraint sparring
  • Coach interruption rounds
  • Unplanned position-start scenarios

Progression

  • Maintain decision quality when the exchange becomes unpredictable
