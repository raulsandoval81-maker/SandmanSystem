Skill 12 — Pressure Mastery

Tree: Pressure Boxing

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Control Space Without Overreaching

B — Build Pressure Through Multiple Systems

C — Sustain Pressure While Remaining Defensively Responsible

Coaching Cues

  • “Pressure without waste”
  • “Make the ring smaller”
  • “Stay safe while making them uncomfortable”

Common Mistakes

  • Using constant forward motion without purpose
  • Neglecting defense during pressure
  • Failing to vary rhythm and level

Drill Ideas

  • Ring-shrinking drill
  • Layered pressure rounds
  • Pressure with defensive return drill

Progression

  • Control space, pace, and reaction through mature pressure boxing
