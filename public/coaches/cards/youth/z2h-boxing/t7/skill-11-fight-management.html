Skill 11 — Fight Management

Tree: Fight Strategy

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Read Time, Pace, Position, And Momentum

B — Choose The Correct Level Of Risk

C — Manage The Round Toward The Desired Outcome

Coaching Cues

  • “Know what the fight needs now”
  • “Not every moment requires the same risk”
  • “Control the round, not just the exchange”

Common Mistakes

  • Fighting every second at maximum pace
  • Ignoring time and score context
  • Taking unnecessary risks while ahead

Drill Ideas

  • Score-scenario rounds
  • Time-management sparring
  • Ahead-behind-even tactical drills

Progression

  • Manage complete rounds with awareness of risk, time, and momentum
