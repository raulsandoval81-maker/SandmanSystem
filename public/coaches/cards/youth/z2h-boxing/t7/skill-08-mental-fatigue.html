Skill 8 — Mental Fatigue

Tree: Fight Psychology

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Create Repeated Decision Demands

B — Change Looks Before The Opponent Adjusts

C — Capitalize When Reactions Slow

Coaching Cues

  • “Make them solve problems every second”
  • “Change the question before they find the answer”
  • “Decision fatigue creates openings”

Common Mistakes

  • Using random actions without purpose
  • Changing tactics too quickly to measure reactions
  • Mistaking physical tiredness for mental fatigue

Drill Ideas

  • Multi-look jab rounds
  • Feint-choice reaction drill
  • Progressive decision-load sparring

Progression

  • Use tactical variety to slow the opponent’s reactions and confidence
