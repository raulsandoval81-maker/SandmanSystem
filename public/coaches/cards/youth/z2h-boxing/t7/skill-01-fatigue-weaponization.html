Skill 1 — Fatigue Weaponization

Tree: Fight Strategy

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Recognize The Opponent’s Fatigue

B — Increase Demands Without Becoming Reckless

C — Force Errors Through Sustained Control

Coaching Cues

  • “Make fatigue work for you”
  • “Raise the demand, not the chaos”
  • “Stay cleaner as they get tired”

Common Mistakes

  • Rushing when the opponent slows
  • Matching the opponent’s poor technique
  • Emptying the gas tank too early

Drill Ideas

  • Fresh-versus-fatigued partner rounds
  • Body-pressure accumulation drill
  • Late-round precision combinations

Progression

  • Use fatigue as a tactical opening while preserving technical control
