Skill 5 — Pressure Layering

Tree: Pressure Boxing

Tier: Youth T7 — Hero

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Begin With Positional Pressure

B — Add Technical And Psychological Threats

C — Sustain Control Across Multiple Exchanges

Coaching Cues

  • “Pressure has more than one layer”
  • “Feet, jab, body, and presence work together”
  • “Build pressure instead of forcing it”

Common Mistakes

  • Using only forward movement
  • Throwing combinations without controlling exits
  • Increasing intensity without maintaining balance

Drill Ideas

  • Foot-pressure to punch-pressure rounds
  • Three-layer pressure drill
  • Partner escape-denial scenarios

Progression

  • Blend positional, technical, and mental pressure into one system
