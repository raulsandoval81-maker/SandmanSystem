Skill 25 — Corner Dictation

Journey: Adult P2L — Boxing Based

Tier: Veteran

Category: Ringcraft

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Guide the opponent into the corner with balanced pressure

B — Dictate the available exit

C — Punish the chosen escape while maintaining position

Coaching Cues

  • “Guide, don’t chase.”
  • “Own the exit.”
  • “Control first, attack second.”

Common Mistakes

  • Following instead of guiding
  • Blocking every exit and smothering the attack
  • Standing square in front of the opponent

Drill Ideas

  • Single-exit corner game
  • Corner guide-and-counter drill
  • Controlled corner-pressure rounds

Progression

  • Connect to Mental Fatigue
  • Connect to High Pressure Rounds
