Skill 24 — Chain Finishing

Journey: Adult P2L — Boxing Based

Tier: Veteran

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Recognize the first break in defense

B — Link attacks across levels and angles

C — End the sequence with control or repositioning

Coaching Cues

  • “Do not stop at the first opening.”
  • “Follow the defense as it changes.”
  • “Finish the chain without losing yourself.”

Common Mistakes

  • Repeating the same punch line
  • Chasing the head while ignoring the body
  • Continuing after position is lost

Drill Ideas

  • Three-response finishing drill
  • Body-head-angle chain
  • Coach-called defensive reaction rounds

Progression

  • Connect to Corner Dictation
  • Connect to Mental Fatigue
