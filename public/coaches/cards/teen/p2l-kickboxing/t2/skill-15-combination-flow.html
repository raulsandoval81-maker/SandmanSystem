Skill 15 — Combination Flow

Journey: Path2Legend™ — Muay Thai

Tier: T2

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Connect punches and kicks without losing posture

B — Change range while staying balanced

C — Finish in guard with a safe angle or exit

Coaching Cues

  • “Every strike has a return.”
  • “Balance before speed.”
  • “Finish facing the partner.”

Common Mistakes

  • Rushing disconnected strikes
  • Falling across the stance
  • Finishing without guard

Drill Ideas

  • Punch-to-kick pad flow
  • Kick-to-punch balance rounds
  • Controlled partner combination flow

Progression

  • Connect to Ring Position
  • Connect to Controlled Sparring
