Skill 07 — Inside Muay Thai

Journey: Path2Legend™ — Muay Thai

Tier: T3

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Enter close range behind a responsible guard and upright posture

B — Establish safe clinch frames before adding controlled knees or short strikes

C — Turn, separate, or reset without losing balance

Coaching Cues

  • “Stay compact.”
  • “Protect while attacking.”
  • “Frame before you strike.”
  • “Control, turn, and reset.”

Common Mistakes

  • Entering without posture or hand position
  • Pulling the partner’s neck instead of controlling position
  • Adding knees before the clinch position is stable
  • Panicking under pressure

Drill Ideas

  • Clinch-frame positioning rounds
  • Controlled frame-to-knee pad drills
  • Clinch turn-and-release exchanges

Progression

  • Connect to Clinch Entry
  • Connect to Advanced Jab
