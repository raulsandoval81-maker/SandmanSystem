Skill 21 — Elbow Fundamentals

Journey: Path2Legend™ — Muay Thai

Tier: T3

Category: Technique

Structure (A/B/C)

A — Build a safe elbow line from balanced Muay Thai posture

B — Keep the opposite hand protecting while the elbow travels

C — Return to guard and position without uncontrolled contact

Coaching Cues

  • “Short path, strong posture.”
  • “Other hand stays home.”
  • “Pad target only.”
  • “Reset under control.”

Common Mistakes

  • Swinging the elbow too wide
  • Dropping the opposite hand
  • Losing posture during rotation
  • Using uncontrolled partner contact

Drill Ideas

  • Elbow-line shadow repetitions
  • Coach-held elbow pad mechanics
  • Controlled frame-to-elbow positioning without head contact
