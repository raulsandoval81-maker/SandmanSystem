Full Live Workflow

Normal Practice

Use the full live workflow when time allows. Check athletes in, build and run the session, verify who actually participated, complete Daily Grind XP and coach input, then close.

Normal Day
  1. 1 Check In Record athletes as they arrive.
  2. 2 Build Session Choose the practice structure.
  3. 3 Run Session Coach the active practice.
  4. 4 Finalize Attendance Verify who actually participated.
  5. 5 Daily Grind XP Award verified attendance XP.
  6. 6 Athlete + Practice Input Record coach observations and practice input.
  7. 7 Notes / Close Finish the record and close the practice.

Builder Bypass

Coach-Directed / Free Day

Skip the Builder when the room needs a coach-led or unstructured practice. Attendance, XP, coach input, and final close still converge on the same practice record.

Free Day
  1. 1 Check In Record athletes as they arrive.
  2. 2 Skip Builder Intentional bypass.
  3. 3 Run Practice Coach the room directly.
  4. 4 Finalize Attendance Confirm the actual participants.
  5. 5 Daily Grind XP Award verified attendance XP.
  6. 6 Athlete + Practice Input Record what mattered from the room.
  7. 7 Notes / Close Finish and close the practice.

After-The-Fact Reconstruction

After-the-Fact / Rush Day

Use this when practice already happened. Skip live check-in and the Builder, recover the actual practice, confirm the date and discipline, select who truly participated, then finish attendance, XP, input, and closeout.

Rush Day
  1. 1 Skip Check-In No fake historical check-in.
  2. 2 Skip Builder The practice already happened.
  3. 3 Recover Practice Create or recover the canonical practice.
  4. 4 Confirm Participants Verify date, discipline, and who actually trained.
  5. 5 Finalize Attendance Lock the verified participant list.
  6. 6 XP + Input Daily Grind XP, athlete input, and practice input.
  7. 7 Notes / Close Complete the practice record and close.
